Enigma — Crescendance

Overview
- This document defines the Enigma Crescendance ability and the "Resonate Mystery" mechanic. Enigma's system is unique to Enigma and her weapons, providing risk/reward stacks that can be consumed for strong effects.

Core Mechanic: Resonate Mystery
- Mechanic: Every 10th click grants 1 stack of "Resonate Mystery". Stacks accumulate and can be consumed individually for small effects or consumed in bulk for a powerful scaling effect when 10+ stacks are consumed.
- Per-stack small consume options (player chooses when consuming a stack):
  - +50% current Notes (applied once on consume)
  - +50 Entropic Melodies
  - +1 of a random minor score you own
  - -75% current Notes (a risk outcome; optional on the small consume pool if you want a gamble mechanic)
- Bulk consume (10 or more stacks):
  - Consume all selected stacks (N stacks) and immediately increase current Notes by 75% * X, where X = number of stacks consumed. Example: consuming 10 stacks gives current Notes += currentNotes * (0.75 * 10) — effectively adding 750% of current Notes (designers: consider making this multiplicative or capped to avoid runaway).

Creator of Mystery Effect
- While Enigma Crescendance is Active:
  - Every 25th click generates 1 additional stack of Resonate Mystery (in addition to the base every-10th stack). This speeds accumulation while the major is active.
- Passive:
  - Every 3rd click will randomly either give +25% current Notes or -25% current Notes. This adds a chaotic flavor (player-facing risk) to Enigma's playstyle; consider making the negative outcome less frequent or gated behind a toggle.

Truthseeker Effect
- While Enigma Crescendance is Active:
  - For each stack of Resonate Mystery consumed, grant 1 minor score of a random minor that the player currently owns (per stack consumed). This makes bulk consumption more reliably rewarding for minor collections.

Duet Effect
- Keep existing Duet wheel mechanics as-is (the wheel and its behaviors remain unchanged).
- Additional Duet behavior for La Campanella interaction (if applicable): while the Duet Effect is active, every time the Grandiose Bell cracks, double your NPC until the Duet ability ends. (This is included here as an explicit cross-reference so Enigma/La Campanella duet interactions are noted.)

Acquisition & Progression Notes
- Resonate Mystery stacks persist in `GameState` and should save across sessions.
- Consider binned storage (e.g., persistent stacks up to a cap, overflow converted to small rewards) to prevent indefinite hoarding.

UI / UX
- Resonate Mystery counter shown on Enigma's weapon panel with a "Consume" action that opens a small choice UI to spend individual stacks for a selected small reward, or consume all stacks when 10+ for the bulk reward.
- On every 10th/25th click grant, show a small visual pulsing ring and a subtle sound to indicate a stack gained.

Implementation Roadmap
1) Add `int ResonateMysteryStacks` to `GameState` and a `int EnigmaClickCounter` for interval checks.
2) Hook main click handler to increment `EnigmaClickCounter` and grant a stack every 10 clicks; if Enigma Crescendance active, also grant an extra stack every 25 clicks.
3) Implement consume handlers: `Enigma_ConsumeStack(GameState, effectChoice)` and `Enigma_ConsumeAllStacks(GameState)` which apply the described effects.
4) Implement `Truthseeker` interaction in `WeaponAbilities.cs` that gives minors per stack consumed when active.
5) Add UI elements: Enigma stack counter, consume UI, and feedback animations/sound.
6) Playtest and tune the negative/positive ratios and the bulk-consume math so it feels rewarding but not broken.

Balancing Notes
- Bulk consume scaling (75% * X) is extremely potent; consider scaling down (e.g., 25% * X) or make it multiplicative with caps (e.g., apply multiplier 1 + min(0.75 * X, 10)).
- The every-3rd-click random +/-25% effect adds volatility — consider a smaller magnitude to reduce player frustration or let the player opt out via a toggle.
