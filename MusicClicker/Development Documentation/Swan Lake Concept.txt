Swan Lake — Concept

Overview
- This is a Swan Lake–specific system: "Swan Feathers" (flavored as Revered, Chromatic, Polyphonic in this concept) and the "Swan Lake Crescendance" ability.
- Feathers are unique to Swan Lake and her weapons. They are collected via clicks and crafting interactions and can be consumed/used to trigger powerful, thematic effects.

Swan Lake Crescendance Ability
- Mechanic: On the main clicker button, at certain click intervals there is a chance to grant one of three feather types: Revered Feather, Chromatic Feather, or Polyphonic Feather.
- Chance intervals:
  - Revered Feather: evaluated every 10 clicks; 5% chance to receive one on that 10th-click event.
  - Chromatic Feather: evaluated every 15 clicks; 5% chance to receive one on that 15th-click event.
  - Polyphonic Feather: evaluated every 30 clicks; (chance may be tuned; default: 5%) evaluated on that 30th-click event.
- Behavioral notes: These chance checks are tied to click counters and are only evaluated on the exact interval clicks (e.g., every 10th/15th/30th click). They are independent checks — a click that is both a 30th and a 15th (i.e., 30th) should consider the rules for the highest-priority feather or all applicable ones depending on design choice. (Recommended: evaluate highest rarity first, i.e., Polyphonic, then Chromatic, then Revered, to avoid multiple simultaneous grants.)

Feather Effects (consume / stacks)
- Revered Feather
  - Stack mechanic: these accumulate in a small counter. At 5 stacks you can click the feather (consume 5 revereds) to instantly give +20% of your current Notes total (adds to your Notes immediately; effect scales with the Notes value at time of use).
  - Flavor: a gentle chorus of feathers adds resonance to your score.

- Chromatic Feather
  - Stack mechanic: accumulate. At 10 stacks you can click the feather (consume 10 chromatics) to instantly give 2 of every minor score (i.e., adds 2 copies/units to each minor you currently own).
  - Flavor: a chromatic flourish grants small guaranteed minors to pad collections and combos.

- Polyphonic Feather
  - Stack mechanic: these are rare; at 1 stack (single Polyphonic) you can click it to instantly give 250 entropic melodies (a high-value resource) and immediately add an additive +75% of your current Notes total (applied once, on consumption).
  - Flavor: a full polyphonic echo collapses into a large, dramatic burst.

Weapon Interactions (Swan-exclusive)

Star-scattered Wings
- Active interaction (requires Swan Lake Major Crescendance active):
  - Whenever you obtain a Revered or Chromatic Feather while the Swan Lake Crescendance Major is active, give +2 of each minor component to one minor score you currently own. (Design detail: pick a random owned minor, or the currently selected one in the UI.)
- Passive (always active):
  - Every 10th click grants +5 Melodious fragments and +5 Harmonious fragments (small passive farming benefit tied to clicks).

Thousand Winged Swan
- Active interaction (requires Swan Lake Major Crescendance active):
  - Whenever you obtain a Polyphonic Feather, for the next 10 seconds your Notes Per Second (NPS) amount is added to your Notes Per Click (NPC) at double rate (i.e., NPC += NPS * 2) for the duration. This effectively temporarily converts ongoing passive income into more potent click value.
- Passive (always active):
  - Each time you acquire a minor score, major score, or musical weapon, immediately increase your current Notes by +50% (additive; applied once on acquisition). This is a strong passive bonus and should be tuned or gated by rarity/cooldown.

Design & Tuning Notes
- Chances are set to 5% at intervals; this can be tuned higher or lower. Polyphonic should be much rarer; consider increasing threshold or adding layering (e.g., Polyphonic only on 30th clicks AND a separate rarity roll).
- Stack and consume mechanics should be visible in the UI (a small Swan panel showing counts for Revered / Chromatic / Polyphonic and a clickable consume button when thresholds are met).
- Weapon Active vs Passive: "With Swan Lake Major Crescendance Active" implies these interactions require you to have the Swan Lake major ability active (or have the Major crafted/resonated). Passive effects apply any time the weapon is owned/equipped.
- Order of evaluation for simultaneous interval ticks: recommend evaluating Polyphonic first (30th), then Chromatic (15th), then Revered (10th) to keep higher-tier feathers prioritized. Alternatively, allow multiple feathers to drop if game balance and code complexity permit.
- Balance: Thousand Winged Swan passive +50% on acquisition is quite strong; consider instead making it +10–25% per acquisition or adding a cooldown or requirement (e.g., effect only triggers once per 60s) to prevent runaway compounding.

UI/Implementation Suggestions
- Create a `Swan Feathers` panel in the Swan Lake weapon card with three counters and a small "Consume" button for each when thresholds are reached.
- Show a tooltip when a feather is granted: "You received a Revered Feather (1/5). Use 5 to receive +20% Notes." Add a small feather animation.
- Clicking a consume button should play a short melody and a feather particle effect.
- Integrate drop feedback into the existing click feedback (floating text, sound cue) so it feels like part of the click loop.

Example Tunings (starting values)
- Revered: 5% every 10th click, cost 5 stacks → +20% Notes (one-shot).
- Chromatic: 5% every 15th click, cost 10 stacks → +2 of each minor.
- Polyphonic: 2% every 30th click, cost 1 → +250 entropic melodies +75% Notes.
- Star-scattered Wings passive: every 10th click → +5 melodious +5 harmonious (tweak counts as needed).
- Thousand Winged Swan passive: +50% on acquisition → consider reducing to +25% or adding cooldown.

Duet Effect (Swan Lake weapons)
- When the Swan Lake Duet effect is activated for a Swan weapon: for the next 10 clicks, each click grants 1 of each feather type (1 Revered, 1 Chromatic, 1 Polyphonic). Additionally, every 5 clicks during the same window grants a bonus +25% to current Notes (applied once at the 5th and 10th click). The Duet ability lasts 10 seconds and has a cooldown of 5 minutes. This Duet NPC grants both immediate feather resources and periodic note bonuses while active.

Open questions
- Should feathers be tradable between weapons or strictly Swan-exclusive? (Current spec: Swan-exclusive.)
- Should thresholds persist across sessions or soft-reset on certain events? (Recommend persist.)
- How should the random roll for feathers interact with other click-based rewards? (Recommend independent, highest-first evaluation.)

Implementation roadmap (next steps)
1) Add counters to `GameState` for `ReveredFeathers`, `ChromaticFeathers`, `PolyphonicFeathers` and click counters for Swan Lake Crescendance.
2) Hook the click handler to evaluate the 10/15/30 interval checks and add feathers accordingly.
3) Add weapon-specific handlers in `WeaponAbilities.cs` for the active interactions on obtaining feathers.
4) Add UI elements in `Views` and hook to `UIUpdater`.
5) Playtest and tune the numbers.
