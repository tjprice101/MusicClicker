EROICA CRESCENDANCE SYSTEM - IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
================================================

STACK NAMES:
- "Heroic Resolve" - gained every time a minor score is crafted
- "Symphonic Catharsis" - consume Heroic Resolve + 25% notes to gain this
- "Testament of Harmony" - Funeral Prayer bond effect stack

MECHANICS:
1. Crescendance (Eroica Major):
   - Every minor craft → +1 Heroic Resolve
   - Consume 1 Heroic Resolve + 25% current notes → +1 Symphonic Catharsis
   - Consume Symphonic Catharsis → 10s of double NPC + 10% crit chance

2. Sakura's Blossom (Eroica I):
   - Passive "Ascending Harmony": Every upgrade bought → +3 to lowest owned upgrade
   - Crescendance Bond "Crimson Requiem": On Catharsis consumption → next 30 clicks get special crit
     - Crit value: +Notes equal to (NPS * NPC)
     - Visual: Deep black text with crimson outline

3. Funeral Prayer (Eroica II):
   - Passive "Melodic Ascension": Every upgrade bought → +2 to lowest owned minor score
   - Crescendance Bond "Testament of Harmony": Each Heroic Resolve consumed → +1 Testament
     - Consume Testament → +100 Melodic and Harmonic Fragments
