La Campanella — Crescendance

Overview
- This doc describes the "La Campanella Crescendance" system and the Grandiose Bell mechanic. It mirrors the style of the Swan Lake and Eroica docs and is intended to be implemented as a La Campanella-exclusive mechanic.
- Core idea: the Grandiose Bell slowly "cracks" as you click; mending the bell at different crack stages yields different, increasingly powerful bonuses. Two La Campanella weapons (Symphony of Bells and Razer of Bells Chimes) interact with these mechanics.

Grandiose Bell (core mechanic)
- Behavior: A persistent bell object associated with La Campanella that tracks clicks toward a "crack counter". The bell cracks at intervals; when cracked you may "mend" it (a UI action) to gain the stage reward and restore the bell to the intact state (counter resets).
- Crack intervals (click thresholds):
  - Crescending Crack — 20 clicks
  - Radiant Crack — 40 clicks
  - Harmonizing Crack — 60 clicks
- Notes: the bell accumulates clicks only while La Campanella is equipped (or Crescendance mode active, design choice). Mending at earlier stages gives weaker rewards; waiting for later stages yields stronger effects but with greater risk of accidentally mending early or losing the opportunity.

Crack Stage Effects (Mend rewards)
- Crescending Crack (20 clicks):
  - Mending at this stage grants you 2 random minor scores that you currently own (i.e., adds +2 to two randomly selected minor score types from your owned set).
  - Purpose: early-stage reward to augment minor collections and encourage short mending intervals.

- Radiant Crack (40 clicks):
  - Mending at this stage grants your next 5 clicks guaranteed Entropic crits. Additionally, each of those 5 clicks grants +1 stack of a temporary stack mechanic called "Deafening Chime".
  - Implementation note: "Entropic crit" should be an existing mechanic or an agreed-upon effect (e.g., a guaranteed critical multiplier to Notes from click). Deafening Chime is a transient stack counter stored in GameState.
  - Purpose: medium-stage tactical reward — a strong click window and stack buildup.

- Harmonizing Crack (60 clicks):
  - Mending at this stage consumes all current stacks of "Deafening Chime" and, for each stack consumed, doubles your current Notes (applied per stack). Example: if you have 3 Deafening Chime stacks, mending consumes them and applies a doubling of your current Notes three times (equivalently multiply Notes by 2^3 = 8). This is a very powerful and explosive end-stage effect and should be tuned or gated.
  - Important: effect order and precision matter — apply doubling atomically and ensure dependent systems (UI smoothing, Triumph of Heroes doubling, etc.) handle the change cleanly.

Timing / Persistence Rules
- Mending always restores the bell to its default (intact) state and resets the crack counter to 0.
- Deafening Chime stacks are transient and stored until consumed by a Harmonizing Mend or cleared by expiry (optional). Recommended: give Deafening Chime stacks a duration of 5–10 minutes or tie them to a short timer (e.g., expire after 10 minutes) to avoid stale accumulation.
- If the player mends before 20 clicks, treat as a normal mending with no crack reward (or block mending until at least 20 clicks reached).

Weapon Interactions

Symphony of Bells
- With La Campanella Crescendance Active (major crafted/resonated):
  - Whenever the Grandiose Bell cracks (at any crack threshold), award an immediate 75% additional total Notes (i.e., multiply current Notes by 1.75 or add 75% of current Notes). This is applied when the crack occurs (not necessarily on mend) — recommended to trigger when the crack threshold is reached, giving a visible bump to the player.
- Passive (Always Active):
  - On craft of a minor score, give an additional copy of that minor score (i.e., craft one minor, receive +1 extra automatically). This encourages minor crafting while wielding this weapon.

Razer of Bells Chimes
- With La Campanella Crescendance Active:
  - When the Grandiose Bell is mended, grant Entropic melodies equal to the number of clicks that elapsed since the bell was last (re)mended. Example: if you mended at 42 clicks, you receive 42 Entropic melodies.
  - Note: this pairs strongly with delaying mends to higher crack stages and can create powerful loops when combined with other effects. Consider adding caps or diminishing returns for extremely high click counts.
- Passive (Always Active):
  - On craft of a minor score, refund 2 random components for that minor (choose two of the required component types uniformly at random and increment their component counts by 2). This passive helps sustain crafting inputs.

Design & Balance Notes
- Harmonizing Crack (stack-based doubling) is extremely strong; recommended mitigations:
  - Add a soft cap or maximum stacks consumed (e.g., consume up to 6 stacks), or
  - Convert doubling to additive or multiplicative with diminishing returns (e.g., +100% per stack but with soft cap), or
  - Require Major Crescendance active to allow the full effect.
- Symphony of Bells' 75% note bonus on crack is powerful if cracks occur often; consider cooldowns or limit frequency (e.g., only the highest crack per mend triggers the 75% bonus).
- Razer of Bells Chimes' entropic reward scales with click count — provide a cap or scale factor (e.g., grant clicks × 0.5 entropic melodies) to avoid runaway gains.

UI / UX Suggestions
- Grandiose Bell UI: show bell icon with a crack meter that fills with clicks; label current click count and next threshold (e.g., "Crack: 15 / 20"). Show visual crack stages at 20/40/60 clicks.
- Mend Action: a dedicated "Mend Bell" button appears when the bell is cracked (and optionally even when intact). Mending triggers an animation and the selected stage's reward.
- Deafening Chime Indicator: small transient stack counter on La Campanella weapon panel showing current Deafening Chime stacks and a tooltip explaining consumption on Harmonizing Mend.
- Feedback: on crack, show the 75% note bonus animation (if Symphony of Bells active) and float the added notes; on mending show entropic melody count (if Razer active) and the resulting doubles.

Suggested GameState fields (implementation-ready)
- `int GrandioseBellClickCounter` — counts clicks toward crack thresholds.
- `int DeafeningChimeStacks` — transient stacks accumulated on Radiant Mend.
- `bool GrandioseBellCracked` or `enum GrandioseBellStage` — current stage (0 = intact, 1 = Crescending, 2 = Radiant, 3 = Harmonizing).
- `DateTime? GrandioseBellLastMend` — optional timestamp to support time-based effects or expiry.

Implementation Roadmap (developer steps)
1) Add fields to `GameState` (see Suggested GameState fields).
2) Hook the main click handler in `MainWindow.axaml.cs` or equivalent to increment `GrandioseBellClickCounter` when La Campanella is equipped/active.
3) When `GrandioseBellClickCounter` reaches 20/40/60, set `GrandioseBellStage` accordingly and trigger on-crack effects (e.g., Symphony 75% bonus if weapon active). Provide UI feedback.
4) Implement a `MendGrandioseBell(GameState)` method that:
   - Applies the reward for the current stage (20/40/60) as described.
   - If Razer of Bells Chimes active, award entropic melodies equal to the click count (or scaled/capped value).
   - Resets `GrandioseBellClickCounter` and `GrandioseBellStage`, clears/handles `DeafeningChimeStacks` as required.
5) Wire Symphony of Bells / Razer of Bells Chimes passive effects to crafting events and to the crack/mend flows in `WeaponAbilities.cs` or the weapons manager.
6) Add UI elements in `Views` and `UIUpdater` for the bell meter, mend button, Deafening Chime stack indicator, and reward notifications.
7) Playtest and tune numbers; add cooldowns/caps if needed to avoid runaway combos.

Tuning suggestions (starting values)
- Crescending crack (20): give 2 random owned minor scores. Keep as-is.
- Radiant crack (40): next 5 clicks guaranteed Entropic crit + +1 Deafening Chime per click. Consider making the Deafening Chime stacks capped at 10.
- Harmonizing crack (60): consume Deafening Chime stacks; for each stack, apply a doubling effect to Notes. Consider capping stacks consumed to 6 or converting to +100% Notes per stack with diminishing returns.
- Symphony of Bells: 75% added total Notes on crack — consider 50% as a safer starting tuning.
- Razer of Bells Chimes: entropic melody grant = clicks at mend, consider scaling by 0.5 or capping at 250.

Open questions for you
- Should Grandiose Bell clicks accumulate only while La Campanella is actively equipped or any time the player clicks while La Campanella is owned? (Recommended: only while equipped to reward active choice.)
- Do you want Deafening Chime stacks to expire over time? (Recommended: yes, set reasonable expiry like 10 minutes.)
- Should Symphony of Bells' passive "give another" on minor craft be limited by cooldown or apply every craft? (Recommend small cooldown (5–30s) if craft frequency is high.)

Duet Effect (additional note)
- While the Duet Effect is active: every time the Grandiose Bell cracks, double your Notes Per Click (NPC) for the duration of the Duet ability. This NPC doubling stacks multiplicatively with other temporary click modifiers and should be removed when the Duet ability ends.
