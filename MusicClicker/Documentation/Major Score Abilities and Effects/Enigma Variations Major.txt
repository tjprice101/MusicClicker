===========================================
ENIGMA VARIATIONS MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"Within the veil of mystery lies infinite possibility—each riddle a key to untold power."

CRESCENDANCE: Resonate Mystery
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Enigma Variations Major must be resonated

Mechanics:
• Every 10th click: +1 Resonant Mystery stack
• Every 15th click (with Creator of Mystery): +1 additional Resonant Mystery stack
  (Total: 2 stacks when both triggers align)

Consumption Options:
• Single Stack Consume (choice of effect):
  - +50% current notes
  - +50 Entropic Melodies
  - +1 random owned minor score
  
• Bulk Consume (10+ stacks):
  - +25% current notes per stack consumed
  - All stacks consumed at once
  - Truthseeker weapon bond grants +1 random minor per 2 stacks consumed

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Creator of Mystery (Weapon I):
• Passive - Mystery of Chaos' Flux (while equipped):
  - Every 3rd click: Randomly ±25% notes
  - 60% chance positive (+25% notes)
  - 40% chance negative (-25% notes)
  - High risk, high reward mechanic
  
• Crescendance Bond - Accelerated Mystery:
  - Every 15th click: +1 additional Resonant Mystery stack
  - Stacks with base every-10th generation
  - Requires: Enigma Major resonated + Creator equipped
  - When triggers align (30th click): grants 2 stacks total

Truthseeker (Weapon II):
• Passive - Revelation Burst (while equipped):
  - On upgrade purchase: gain +5 Resonant Mystery stacks
  - Accelerates mystery stack generation through progression
  - Passive effect, requires equipped
  
• Crescendance Bond - Knowledge Harvest:
  - For every 2 Resonant Mystery stacks consumed: +1 random owned minor
  - Applies to both single and bulk consumption
  - Requires: Enigma Major resonated + Truthseeker equipped
  - Example: Consume 10 stacks = 5 random minors granted

DUET ABILITY: Mystery Clicks (Creator + Truthseeker)
---------------------------------------
Click-based resource generation system:
• Clicks grant random Red or Blue mystery clicks (50/50 chance)
• Red Mystery Click Effect: +5× current NPS as notes
• Blue Mystery Click Effect: +10% NPS (stacks additively, permanent)
• Duration: 30 seconds
• Cooldown: 3 minutes

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Stack 10+ Resonate Mystery before bulk consuming for better value
2. Truthseeker makes bulk consume powerful (+1 minor per 2 stacks)
3. Creator of Mystery passive is risky - monitor note total
4. Mystery Clicks Red effect provides instant burst, Blue provides long-term growth
5. Save Mystery Clicks for active clicking sessions to maximize both effects
6. Truthseeker passive grants mystery stacks on every upgrade purchase

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Resonate Mystery generation is slow (every 10 clicks)
• Single consume for immediate needs (+50% notes or entropic)
• Mystery Clicks duet provides simple Red/Blue choice

Mid Game:
• Creator weapon accelerates stack generation (every 15th click bonus)
• Truthseeker bulk consume generates minors efficiently (1 per 2 stacks)
• Truthseeker passive grants +5 stacks on every upgrade purchase
• Mystery Clicks becomes more valuable with higher NPS

Late Game:
• Bulk consume with Truthseeker = steady minor score growth
• 20+ stack bulk consume grants 10 random minors
• Blue Mystery Clicks stack for massive permanent NPS increases
• Chaotic Flux ±25% becomes less risky with larger note pools

SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------------
Best Paired With:
• Eroica: Bulk consume minors + Victory March doubling = exponential
• Ode to Joy: Mystery-generated minors trigger Petal of Harmony
• La Campanella: Symphony duplication doubles mystery-granted minors

Avoid Pairing With:
• Swan Lake: Both RNG-dependent - inconsistent combo
• Moonlight: Nighttime restriction conflicts with 10-click cadence

BALANCE NOTES
---------------------------------------
Version 1.2 - Bulk Consume Nerf:
• Reduced bulk consume from +75% → +25% notes per stack
• Reason: 75% per stack created exponential scaling issues
• Truthseeker bonus unchanged (still +1 minor per stack)

Version 1.0 - Initial Implementation:
• Mystery Clicks Black effect originally -50%, increased to -65% for risk balance
• Creator Chaotic Flux originally 50/50 distribution, adjusted to 60/40 positive bias

ADVANCED TECHNIQUES
---------------------------------------
Stack Management:
• Single consume at <10 stacks for immediate needs
• Bulk consume at 10-20 stacks for optimal value
• With Truthseeker: 20-stack bulk = 20 random minors + (20 × 25% notes)

Risk Mitigation:
• Creator Chaotic Flux averages to +5% per 3 clicks over time (60% × 25% - 40% × 25%)
• Mystery Clicks: Click slowly to limit Black exposure (only 10s duration)
• Save game before using Mystery Clicks if risk-averse

Purple Effect Optimization:
• Purple grants +1 per owned major type (can grant 7+ majors in late game)
• Use Mystery Clicks when you own many major types for maximum value
