===========================================
FATE (SYMPHONY NO. 5) MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"The stars themselves bend to your will, weaving cosmic threads into a tapestry of destiny."

CRESCENDANCE: Cosmic Modulation
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Fate Major must be resonated

Mechanics:
• Every 8th click: +1 Cosmic Modulation stack + 10% current notes
• Immediate +10% note bonus per stack gained

Tier System (every 5 stacks):
• Tier 1 (1-4 stacks): Increased entropic crit chance
• Tier 2 (5-9 stacks): Double NPS passive effect
• Tier 3 (10+ stacks): Guaranteed Crescending crits on clicks

Consumption:
• Consume stacks for Entropic Melodies (+15 per stack consumed)
• Can consume any amount (1-all stacks)
• Consuming removes tier benefits

Symphony of the Stars:
• Special resource generated via Astral Chainripper weapon bond
• Consume 1 Symphony: +3 to lowest owned minor score
• Cosmic Weaver bond: Also grants 5 guaranteed entropic crit clicks

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Astral Chainripper (Weapon I):
• Passive - Temporal Surge (while equipped):
  - On Fate minor score craft: 5× NPS for 10 seconds
  - Massive temporary passive income boost
  - Stackable duration (multiple crafts extend timer)
  
• Crescendance Bond - Stellar Convergence:
  - Every 5 Cosmic Modulation stacks gained:
    → Automatically grants +1 Symphony of the Stars
  - Requires: Fate Major resonated + Astral Chainripper equipped
  - Passive generation, no manual activation needed

Cosmic Weaver (Weapon II):
• Passive - Harmonic Multiplication (while equipped):
  - On any minor craft: If you own 1+ of that major, gain +3 of that major score
  - Accelerates major score collection significantly
  - Works for ALL minor crafts (not just Fate)
  
• Crescendance Bond - Entropic Starfall:
  - Each Symphony of the Stars consumed:
    → Next 5 clicks are guaranteed Entropic Melody critical hits
  - Requires: Fate Major resonated + Cosmic Weaver equipped
  - Stacks with multiple Symphony consumptions

DUET ABILITY: Hourglass Reversal (Astral Chainripper + Cosmic Weaver)
---------------------------------------
Time-based banking and replay mechanic:
• First 10 seconds: Bank all actions (clicks, notes gained, etc.)
• Last 10 seconds: Replay at X× effectiveness (X = number of clicks performed)

Mechanics:
• Click count during first 10s determines multiplier for second 10s
• All actions (notes, stacks, etc.) are recorded and replayed
• More clicks in first half = stronger replay in second half

Example:
• First 10s: Perform 20 clicks, gain 1M notes
• Last 10s: Replay grants 20M notes (20× multiplier from 20 clicks)

Duration: 20 seconds (10s banking + 10s replay)
Cooldown: 8 minutes

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Every 5th click grants stack - extremely consistent generation
2. Don't consume Cosmic stacks unless you need entropic melodies
3. Tier 3 (10+ stacks) provides guaranteed crits - very powerful
4. Symphony of the Stars best used on lowest minor for balanced growth
5. Cosmic Weaver passive works on ALL minors - equip early
6. Hourglass Reversal: Click as much as possible in first 10 seconds

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Cosmic Modulation stacks slowly but consistently
• +10% notes per stack provides steady growth
• Astral Chainripper 5× NPS boost helps early progression

Mid Game:
• Tier 2 double NPS activates at 5 stacks (very achievable)
• Symphony of the Stars generation begins with Astral Chainripper
• Harmonic Multiplication accelerates major collection
• Hourglass Reversal becomes available - powerful burst tool

Late Game:
• Tier 3 (10+ stacks) = all clicks are entropic crits
• Symphony stacks can reach 10+ for massive minor boosts
• Entropic Starfall grants 5 guaranteed crits per Symphony (50+ crits possible)
• Hourglass Reversal with 50+ click multiplier = exponential replay

SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------------
Best Paired With:
• Eroica: Cosmic Modulation Tier 3 + Crimson Requiem = guaranteed crit burst
• La Campanella: Guaranteed crits accelerate Deafening Chime stacking
• Ode to Joy: Entropic crits trigger Joyful Catharsis passive

Avoid Pairing With:
• Winter: NPS freeze negates Tier 2 double NPS benefit
• Moonlight: Nighttime restriction doesn't synergize with 5-click cadence

ADVANCED TECHNIQUES
---------------------------------------
Cosmic Stack Management:
• Maintain 10+ stacks for Tier 3 guaranteed crits
• Only consume for entropic when you have 15+ stacks (surplus)
• Astral Chainripper generates 1 Symphony per 5 Cosmic (optimal at 10 Cosmic)

Symphony Optimization:
• Save Symphony stacks for critical moments
• With Cosmic Weaver: Consume all Symphonies before major clicking session
• 5 Symphonies = 25 guaranteed entropic crits (5 per Symphony)

Hourglass Strategy:
• First 10s: Click speed determines multiplier - use click speed buffs
• First 10s: Consume resources, activate buffs - all will replay
• Last 10s: Don't click excessively - replay handles the work
• Optimal: 30-50 clicks in first 10s for 30-50× replay multiplier

Tier Exploitation:
• Tier 3 (10+ stacks) makes every click an entropic crit
• Pair with Eroica Crimson Requiem for 30 guaranteed entropic burst clicks
• Pair with La Campanella bell progression for entropic Deafening stacks
