===========================================
ODE TO JOY (SYMPHONY NO. 9) MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"From petals of harmony and melody, life itself blooms—a symphony of boundless celebration."

CRESCENDANCE: Petals of Life
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Ode to Joy Major must be resonated

Petal Generation:
• Minor craft: +1 Petal of Harmony
• Major craft: +1 Petal of Melody
• Both trigger Joyful Catharsis bond (double NPS for 5s)

Petal Consumption:
• Consume Petal of Harmony: +83 Entropic Melodies
• Consume Petal of Melody: +10 seconds of guaranteed Entropic Crescendo of Eternity critical hits (stackable)

Ode to Life Crafting:
• Recipe: 1 Petal of Harmony + 1 Petal of Melody + 17 Entropic Melodies
• Result: 1 Ode to Life stack
• Consume Ode to Life: Double all owned minor scores
• Ode to Creation bond: Doubles passive petal generation for 25s

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Joyful Catharsis (Weapon I):
• Passive - Harmonic Entropy (while equipped):
  - Every 50th click: +X Entropic Melodies (X = critical notes gotten × 3)
  - Scales with your critical hit performance
  - Tracks cumulative crit performance, not just recent
  
• Crescendance Bond - Euphoric Resonance:
  - Every time you gain any Petal: Double NPS for 5 seconds
  - Effect stacks with multiple petal gains
  - Requires: Ode to Joy Major resonated + Joyful Catharsis equipped
  - Applies to both Harmony and Melody petals

Ode to Creation (Weapon II):
• Passive - Rhythmic Genesis (while equipped):
  - Every 20th click: Generate random Petal of Harmony OR Petal of Melody
  - 50/50 chance for either petal type
  - Passive petal generation independent of crafting
  
• Crescendance Bond - Life Amplification:
  - On Ode to Life consume: Reduce passive effect by 10 clicks to activate for 25 seconds
  - Generates petals every 10th click instead of 20th
  - Requires: Ode to Joy Major resonated + Ode to Creation equipped
  - Temporary boost to petal generation rate

DUET ABILITY: Crescendo Conductor (Joyful Catharsis + Ode to Creation)
---------------------------------------
Build 16-note melodies through clicking with escalating rewards:
• 4 notes: +25 Melodious + 25 Harmonious Fragments
• 8 notes: +5 Petals of Harmony
• 12 notes: +5 Petals of Melody
• 16 notes: +1 Ode to Life

Mechanics:
• Repeatable/stackable - can complete multiple 16-note cycles
• Counter resets after 16 notes, immediately starts new cycle
• On ability expiry: +3 Entropic Melodies per completed 4-note section

Duration: 20 seconds
Cooldown: 10 minutes

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Petals generate from ANY minor/major craft - not just Ode to Joy
2. Save Petals of Melody for critical moments (Entropic Crescendo of Eternity windows)
3. Ode to Life doubling is most powerful mid-game (exponential minor growth)
4. Joyful Catharsis rewards crit-heavy playstyle
5. Ode to Creation provides passive petals - equip early
6. Crescendo Conductor: Click rapidly to maximize 16-note completions

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Petal generation is slow (requires crafting)
• Ode to Creation passive helps accelerate petal collection
• Focus on Harmony petals for entropic melodies

Mid Game:
• Ode to Life crafting becomes viable (requires 17 entropic)
• Doubling minors creates exponential growth
• Euphoric Resonance double NPS stacks provide nice boosts
• Crescendo Conductor grants reliable major sheets

Late Game:
• Multiple Ode to Life consumes = repeated minor doubling
• Petal of Melody stacking creates permanent Entropic Crescendo of Eternity windows
• Harmonic Entropy generates 100+ entropic per 50 clicks with high crit rate
• Life Amplification doubles petal generation (every 10 clicks)

SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------------
Best Paired With:
• Eroica: Victory March doubling + Ode to Life doubling = quad minors
• La Campanella: Symphony duplication + Ode to Life = explosive growth
• Fate: Tier 3 guaranteed crits maximize Harmonic Entropy entropic gain

Avoid Pairing With:
• Winter: NPS freeze negates Euphoric Resonance double NPS benefit
• Swan Lake: Both require crafting focus - compete for resources

BALANCE NOTES

ADVANCED TECHNIQUES
---------------------------------------
Ode to Life Multiplication:
• First consume: 10 minors → 20 minors
• Second consume: 20 minors → 40 minors
• Third consume: 40 minors → 80 minors
• Exponential growth - save multiple Ode to Life stacks for burst

Petal of Melody Stacking:
• Each Melody = +10s of guaranteed Entropic Crescendo of Eternity critical hits
• 5 Melody petals = 50s continuous Entropic Crescendo of Eternity critical hits
• Stack duration before consuming for extended crit windows
• Pair with Fate Tier 3 or Eroica Crimson Requiem for mega-crits

Crescendo Conductor Optimization:
• 20 seconds = ~40-60 clicks possible
• Can complete 2-3 full 16-note cycles per use
• Each cycle grants 5× NPS for 15s (stacks duration)
• Final entropic bonus: 12-18 entropic (3 per completed 4-note section)

Life Amplification Exploitation:
• Ode to Creation bond: Doubles generation for 25s (every 10 clicks)
• During 25s window: Can gain 2-3 extra petals
• Chain multiple Ode to Life consumes for extended double generation
• Use during Crescendo Conductor for maximum petal gain

Harmonic Entropy Maximization:
• Every 50 clicks: Entropic = (critical notes × 3)
• With Fate Tier 3: All clicks are crits = 50 crit notes per 50 clicks
• Potential: 50 entropic per 50 clicks (50 × 1)
• Requires high crit rate or guaranteed crit buffs (Fate/Eroica)
