===========================================
WINTER MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"Where frozen silence crystallizes into power—the eternal blizzard commands absolute dominion over time itself."

MAJOR ABILITY: Glacial Dominion
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Winter Major must be acquired

Mechanics:
• Grants bonus effects when NPS is frozen
• Passive effect - always active when Winter Major owned
• Synergizes with Crescendance freeze mechanics

CRESCENDANCE: Requiem of the Frozen Choir
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Winter Major must be resonated

Core Mechanic - Freezing Harmony:
• Button: Freeze NPS for 5 seconds (NPS value locked, no passive notes gained)
• Cooldown: 3 seconds
• Effect: +1 Frigid Melody stack per use

Stack System Overview:
• Frigid Melody: Primary resource (gained from Freezing Harmony)
• Eternal Frost: Offensive branch (critical hits + enhanced clicks)
• Regal Snowlight: Utility branch (fragments + entropic melodies)

Frigid Melody Consumption:
• Button: "Ignite the Blizzard" - converts Frigid Melody into branch stacks
• Consume X Frigid Melody → +X Eternal Frost OR +X Regal Snowlight
• Player chooses which branch to empower

ETERNAL FROST (Offensive Branch):
• Consume Button: "Unleash Frozen Wrath"
• Consumes all Eternal Frost stacks
• Effect 1: Freeze NPS for 5 seconds
• Effect 2: Next 15 clicks per stack consumed are enhanced
  - Enhanced clicks stack: 1 stack = 15 clicks, 5 stacks = 75 clicks, etc.
  - Enhanced clicks use special crit: "Blizzard's Command of Eternal Ice"
  - Formula: (NPS * NPC)^2
  - Extremely powerful with high NPS values

REGAL SNOWLIGHT (Utility Branch):
• Consume Button: "Invoke Crystalline Grace"
• Consumes all Regal Snowlight stacks
• Effect per stack consumed:
  - +50 Melodious Fragments
  - +50 Harmonious Fragments
  - +25 Entropic Melodies

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Cacophonic Blizzard (Weapon I):
• Passive - Permafrost Strikes (while equipped):
  - Every 20th click: Freeze NPS for 5 seconds (like Freezing Harmony)
  - Every 20th click: Next 20 clicks gain +50% notes each
  - Passive freeze effect works independently of Crescendance
  
• Crescendance Bond - Snow's Oblivion:
  - When Eternal Frost consumed during Crescendance:
    → +2 Snow's Oblivion stacks
  - Consume 1 Snow's Oblivion:
    → +X Entropic Melodies (X = total upgrades owned)
  - Requires: Winter Major resonated + Cacophonic Blizzard equipped
  - Scales with progression (more upgrades = more entropic)

The Snow's Desire (Weapon II):
• Passive - Accelerating Flurry (while equipped):
  - Every click: +1% NPC (stacks up to +50% max)
  - Decay: After 5 seconds of no clicking, bonus resets to 0%
  - Active clicking bonus - rewards rapid clicking
  - Display: Shows current bonus % in Crescendance panel
  
• Crescendance Bond - Frigid Resonance:
  - Whenever you gain 1 Frigid Melody stack:
    → Also gain +1 bonus Frigid Melody stack (doubling effect)
  - Requires: Winter Major resonated + The Snow's Desire equipped
  - Doubles Freezing Harmony efficiency (1 freeze = 2 stacks instead of 1)

DUET ABILITY: Symphony of Absolute Zero
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Winter Major resonated
• Both Cacophonic Blizzard AND The Snow's Desire equipped

Mechanics:
• Freeze NPS for 20 seconds
• Every click during freeze:
  - Grants +1 Frigid Melody stack
  - Grants "Blizzard's Command of Eternal Ice" crit (NPC × NPS bonus)
  - Adds +0.5 seconds to freeze duration (max +10 seconds total)
• Maximum Duration: 30 seconds (20 base + 10 from clicks)

Cooldown: 4 minutes

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Freeze NPS strategically - converts passive income to click power
2. Build Frigid Melody stacks, then choose Frost (offense) or Snowlight (utility)
3. Eternal Frost crits scale with NPS - freeze at high NPS for max damage
4. Regal Snowlight best for fragment/entropic farming
5. The Snow's Desire doubles Frigid Melody gain - equip for faster stacking
6. Cacophonic Blizzard's passive 20th-click freeze combos with main Crescendance
7. Snow's Oblivion scales with upgrades - more powerful in late game
8. Accelerating Flurry requires active clicking - 50% NPC bonus is massive

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Use Freezing Harmony to build Frigid Melody (freeze NPS every 3s)
• Focus on Regal Snowlight branch for fragments + entropic
• Cacophonic Blizzard passive provides free freezes every 20 clicks

Mid Game:
• Eternal Frost becomes viable as NPS increases
• Blizzard crits (NPC × NPS) grow exponentially stronger
• Snow's Oblivion grants scaling entropic based on upgrade count
• Start alternating between Frost/Snowlight based on needs

Late Game:
• Eternal Frost is primary offensive tool (massive crit values)
• The Snow's Desire equipped doubles Frigid Melody gain
• Duet (Symphony of Absolute Zero) provides 20-30s burst window
• Accelerating Flurry +50% NPC bonus is game-changing
• Snow's Oblivion grants hundreds/thousands of entropic per consume
