===========================================
DIES IRAE MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"Wrath incarnate—where burning hatred forges an infernal symphony of unstoppable retribution."

MAJOR ABILITY: Infernal Ascension
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Dies Irae Major must be acquired

• Clicks build infernal power that multiplies damage
• Passive effect - always active when Dies Irae Major owned
• Synergizes with Crescendance hatred stacking system

CRESCENDANCE: Symphony of Hell's Retribution
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Dies Irae Major must be resonated

Stack Mechanics:
• Every click: +1 Burning Hatred (up to 50 cap)
• After 50 Burning Hatred: Additional clicks grant +1 Discordant Malice instead
• No cap on Discordant Malice stacks

Burning Hatred Consumption:
• Consume 5 Burning Hatred: +1 Dissonant Oblivion stack
• Reduces Burning Hatred by 5 per Oblivion gained
• Manual conversion via button

Discordant Malice Consumption:
• Consume stacks for Entropic Melodies
• Entropic gained = X (X = current Burning Hatred stacks)
• Example: 30 Burning Hatred, consume 5 Malice = 150 Entropic (30 × 5)

Dissonant Oblivion Consumption:
• Consume 1 Oblivion: Next 20 clicks are "Symphony of Hell's Retribution" crits
• Hell crit formula: NPC × NPS × Burning Hatred stacks
• Extremely powerful with high Burning Hatred and NPS

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Seven Circles (Weapon I):
• Passive - Wrathful Amplification (while equipped):
  - Every critical hit: +20% current notes
  - Applies to ALL crit types (entropic, hell, etc.)
  - Passive effect, always active
  
• Crescendance Bond - Seal Resonance:
  - Symphony of Hell's Retribution crits: +1 Wrathful Seal per crit
  - Consume 1 Wrathful Seal: Next 5 clicks are "Seal-breaking" clicks
  - Seal-breaking clicks grant massive damage
  - Requires: Dies Irae Major resonated + Seven Circles equipped

Hell's Wrath (Weapon II):
• Passive - Infernal Bounty (while equipped):
  - Every 50th click: +2 to your 2 lowest owned minor scores
  - Passive minor generation from clicking
  - Helps balance minor score collection
  
• Crescendance Bond - Entropic Fury:
  - Symphony of Hell's Retribution crits: +200 Entropic Melodies per crit
  - Requires: Dies Irae Major resonated + Hell's Wrath equipped
  - Combines with base Oblivion consumption for massive entropic gain

DUET ABILITY: (Not Yet Implemented)
---------------------------------------
Dies Irae duet ability is planned but not yet implemented in current version.

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Build Burning Hatred to 50 before consuming for Oblivion
2. Dissonant Oblivion crits scale with BOTH NPC and NPS - maximize both
3. Save Oblivion consumes for when you have high Burning Hatred (30-50)
4. Discordant Malice consumption scales with Burning Hatred - strategic timing
5. Hell's Wrath provides passive minors - equip for long clicking sessions
6. Seven Circles crit bonus stacks multiplicatively - extremely powerful

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Burning Hatred builds slowly (1 per click to 50 cap)
• Focus on building to 50 before first Oblivion conversion
• Wrathful Amplification provides consistent +20% per crit

Mid Game:
• Discordant Malice stacking begins after 50 Burning Hatred
• Oblivion crits become viable (NPC × NPS × Burning Hatred)
• Infernal Bounty minors help progression
• Entropic Fury generates massive entropic from Oblivion crits

Late Game:
• Hell crit formula becomes exponential (high NPC × NPS × 50 Hatred)
• 20 Hell crits from 1 Oblivion = massive note gain
• Wrathful Seal stacking creates Seal-breaking burst windows
• Discordant Malice → Entropic conversion generates 1000+ entropic per consume

SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------------
Best Paired With:
• Eroica: Crimson Requiem boosts NPC, Hell crits benefit massively
• Fate: High NPS from Tier 2 boosts Hell crit formula (NPC × NPS × Hatred)
• Moonlight: Nighttime NPC bonuses enhance Hell crit damage

Avoid Pairing With:
• Winter: NPS freeze negates Hell crit formula scaling (NPC × NPS × Hatred)
• Swan Lake: Both require consistent clicking - compete for attention

BALANCE NOTES
---------------------------------------

• Hell crit formula (NPC × NPS × Hatred) provides exponential late-game power
• Wrathful Seal and Seal-breaking mechanics add strategic depth

ADVANCED TECHNIQUES
---------------------------------------
Hatred Management:
• Optimal: Maintain 40-50 Burning Hatred at all times
• Convert to Oblivion in increments of 5 (consume 5 Hatred per Oblivion)
• Never let Burning Hatred drop below 30 (reduces Hell crit effectiveness)

Oblivion Timing:
• Save Oblivion for high NPS moments (Fate Tier 2, Moonlight Full Moon, etc.)
• Hell crit formula: NPC × NPS × Burning Hatred
• Example: 1000 NPC × 10,000 NPS × 50 Hatred = 500,000,000 per crit
• 20 Hell crits from 1 Oblivion = 10,000,000,000 total damage

Discordant Malice Strategy:
• Let Malice stacks accumulate to 10+ before consuming
• Entropic gain = Malice consumed × Burning Hatred
• Example: 10 Malice × 50 Hatred = 500 Entropic Melodies
• Only consume when Burning Hatred is maxed (50)

Wrathful Seal Exploitation:
• Each Hell crit grants +1 Wrathful Seal (20 crits = 20 Seals)
• Consume Seals for Seal-breaking clicks (5 per Seal)
• Chain: Oblivion (20 Hell crits) → 20 Seals → 100 Seal-breaking clicks
• Seal-breaking damage formula not yet documented (high damage assumed)

NPC/NPS Maximization:
• Hell crit formula multiplicative - boost BOTH NPC and NPS
• Eroica Crimson Requiem: Boosts NPC significantly
• Fate Tier 2: Doubles NPS
• Moonlight duet: 2× NPC + 3× NPS during all phases
• Combined with 50 Hatred: Astronomical Hell crit damage

CRITICAL WARNINGS
---------------------------------------
1. DO NOT consume all Burning Hatred - maintain 30+ for Hell crit effectiveness
2. Dissonant Oblivion is most powerful resource - use strategically
3. Discordant Malice consumption worthless if Burning Hatred is low
4. Seven Circles Wrathful Amplification is multiplicative - prioritize crits
5. Hell's Wrath Entropic Fury can generate 4000+ entropic from 1 Oblivion (20 crits × 200)
