===========================================
SWAN LAKE MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"Through graceful wings, fortune's feathers descend—each one a promise of transcendent beauty."

MAJOR ABILITY: Feather Bounty
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Swan Lake Major must be acquired

Mechanics:
• Grants bonus components when feathers are collected
• Passive effect - always active when Swan Lake Major owned
• Works in conjunction with Crescendance feather system

CRESCENDANCE: Wings of Transcendence
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Swan Lake Major must be resonated

Mechanics:
• Revered Feathers (every 2 clicks):
  - Consume 5 stacks → +20% current notes
  
• Chromatic Feathers (every 8 clicks):
  - Consume 10 stacks → +2 of every owned minor score
  
• Polyphonic Feathers (every 15 clicks):
  - Consume 1 stack → +83 Entropic Melodies + 75% current notes

Feather Priority:
• Polyphonic checked first (highest priority)
• Then Chromatic
• Then Revered
• Only ONE feather drops per eligible click

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Star-Scattered Wings (Weapon I):
• Forte Resonance - Stellar Fragment Rain (while equipped):
  - Every 10th click: +5 Melodious + 5 Harmonious Fragments
  - Passive fragment generation independent of feathers
  
• Crescendance Bond - Stellar Component Rain:
  - When Revered or Chromatic Feather obtained during Crescendance:
    → +2 components to random owned minor score
  - Requires: Swan Lake Major resonated + Star-Scattered equipped
  - Auto-triggers on feather acquisition

Thousand Winged Swan (Weapon II):
• Forte Resonance - Wings of Fortune (while equipped):
  - On weapon acquisition: +50% notes instantly (one-time)
  - First-time acquisition bonus only
  
• Crescendance Bond - Wings of Velocity:
  - When Polyphonic Feather consumed during Crescendance:
    → 10 seconds of NPS-to-NPC boost (adds 2× NPS to NPC) -> new special crit: "Dawn of the Swan's Glory" in a white text with a pink outline. 
  - Requires: Swan Lake Major resonated + Thousand Winged equipped
  - Extremely powerful for click-heavy strategies

DUET ABILITY: Feather Cascade (Star-Scattered + Thousand Winged)
---------------------------------------
First 10 clicks rain all feather types with milestone bonuses:
• Each of first 10 clicks: Grants 1 Revered + 1 Chromatic + 1 Polyphonic Feather
• Every 5th click (5th & 10th): +25% current notes bonus

Total Feather Gain:
• 10 Revered (can consume 2× for +40% notes)
• 10 Chromatic (can consume 1× for +2 all minors)
• 10 Polyphonic (can consume for massive entropic + notes)

Duration: 20 seconds (but typically finishes in ~10 clicks)
Cooldown: 4 minutes

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Feathers drop on consistent intervals (every 2/8/15 clicks)
2. Revered is easiest to stack (every 2 clicks vs 8/15)
3. Save Polyphonic for critical moments - most powerful feather
4. Chromatic best used mid-game for minor score acceleration
5. Feather Cascade duet = instant 30 feathers total
6. Thousand Winged NPS-to-NPC boost stacks with other click multipliers

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Feather drops are rare - focus on other crescendance systems
• Revered feathers provide consistent small note boosts
• Star-Scattered fragments help with early upgrades

Mid Game:
• Chromatic feathers accelerate minor score collection (every 8 clicks)
• Feather Cascade becomes available - use strategically
• Thousand Winged acquisition bonus provides nice one-time boost

Late Game:
• Polyphonic feathers (every 15 clicks) with NPS-to-NPC boost create massive burst windows
• Feather Cascade generates 83-830 Entropic Melodies instantly
• Combine Chromatic consumption (+2 all minors) with Ode to Joy doubling effects
• RNG feather drops become more forgiving with higher click frequency

SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------------
Best Paired With:
• Eroica: Chromatic feather minor boosts + Victory March = exponential scaling
• Fate: NPS-to-NPC boost benefits Cosmic Modulation clicks
• Enigma: Feather Cascade generates fragments for mystery consumption

Avoid Pairing With:
• Winter: NPS freeze negates Thousand Winged NPS-to-NPC boost
• Dies Irae: Feather RNG unreliable for consistent Burning Hatred stacking

BALANCE NOTES
---------------------------------------
Version 1.0 - Initial Implementation:
• Feather drop rates balanced at 5% to avoid excessive RNG frustration
• Polyphonic is intentionally rare (every 30 clicks) due to power level
• Duet ability compensates for RNG by providing guaranteed feather burst
