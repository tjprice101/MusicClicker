===========================================
LA CAMPANELLA MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"Each chime resonates with destiny; as the bell cracks, fortune rings eternal."

MAJOR ABILITY: Bell's Fortune
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• La Campanella Major must be acquired

Mechanics:
• Grants bonus +10% bonus notes whenever the Grandiose Bell cracks
• Passive effect - always active when La Campanella Major owned
• Works in conjunction with Crescendance bell system

CRESCENDANCE: Grandiose Bell
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• La Campanella Major must be resonated

Mechanics:
Bell progresses through stages based on click count:
• Stage 0 (Intact): 0-19 clicks
• Stage 1 (Crescending): 20-39 clicks
• Stage 2 (Radiant): 40-59 clicks
• Stage 3 (Harmonizing): 60+ clicks

Bell cracks at 20, 40, and 60 clicks, advancing to next stage.

Mending Rewards:
• Crescending (Stage 1): +2 random owned minor scores
• Radiant (Stage 2): Next 5 clicks are Entropic Crescendo of Eternity critical hits + 5 Deafening Chime stacks 
• Harmonizing (Stage 3): Consume all Deafening Chime for 2^stacks multiplier to notes

Deafening Chime Mechanics:
• Gained from Radiant mend (5 stacks) or Entropic Crescendo of Eternity critical hits (3 stacks)
• Max effective stacks for Harmonizing mend: 6 (2^6 = 64× multiplier)
• Stacks have 2-minute expiry timer
• Can stack from multiple Radiant mends before Harmonizing consumption

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Symphony of Bells (Weapon I):
• Passive - Harmonic Duplication (while equipped):
  - On minor craft: Duplicate that minor (+1 extra copy)
  - Every minor craft grants 2× the score
  - Passive effect, requires equipped
  
• Crescendance Bond - Resonant Crack Bonus:
  - When Grandiose Bell cracks (at 20/40/60 clicks):
    → Instantly gain +75% current notes
  - Requires: La Campanella Major resonated + Symphony equipped
  - Triggers at each crack threshold

Razer of Bell's Chimes (Weapon II):
• Passive - Component Echo (while equipped):
  - On minor craft: Refund 2 random components for that minor
  - Helps offset crafting costs
  - Passive effect, requires equipped
  
• Crescendance Bond - Entropic Resonance:
  - On Grandiose Bell mend (any stage):
    → Gain Entropic Melodies = click count × 2/3 (max 83)
  - Requires: La Campanella Major resonated + Razer equipped
  - Maximum 83 Entropic per mend even if 60+ clicks

DUET ABILITY: Chime Chain (Symphony + Razer)
---------------------------------------
Build exponential click chains within tight time windows:
• Each click within 1 second of previous click continues the chain
• Chain breaks if 1+ seconds pass without clicking
• Reward calculation: (chain length)² × NPS as instant notes

Example Chains:
• 5-click chain: 25 × NPS instant notes
• 10-click chain: 100 × NPS instant notes
• 20-click chain: 400 × NPS instant notes

Duration: 10 seconds
Cooldown: 10 minutes

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Plan bell mending strategically - don't mend at Crescending unless desperate
2. Stack multiple Radiant mends (5 Deafening each) before Harmonizing mend
3. 6 Deafening Chime stacks = 64× multiplier (optimal target)
4. Symphony weapon doubles ALL minor crafts - extremely powerful
5. Razer entropic generation scales with click count (aim for 60+ before mending)
6. Chime Chain requires fast clicking - practice timing for maximum chains

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Bell system is slow - requires 60 clicks for Harmonizing mend
• Symphony duplication helps accelerate minor collection
• Component Echo reduces crafting costs

Mid Game:
• Radiant mends provide reliable Entropic Crescendo of Eternity critical hit windows
• Deafening Chime stacking becomes viable strategy
• Resonant Crack Bonus provides consistent note boosts every 20 clicks

Late Game:
• Harmonizing mend with 6 Deafening = 64× note multiplier
• Can stack 12+ Deafening from multiple Radiant mends (diminishing returns after 6)
• Chime Chain with high NPS creates massive instant note gains
• Pair with click speed buffs for faster bell progression

SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------------
Best Paired With:
• Eroica: Symphony duplication + Victory March doubling = exponential minors
• Ode to Joy: Symphony duplication accelerates Petal of Harmony generation
• Moonlight: Nighttime clicking + Resonant Crack Bonus = consistent bursts

Avoid Pairing With:
• Winter: NPS freeze reduces Chime Chain effectiveness
• Swan Lake: Both rely on RNG/specific mechanics - better to alternate

BALANCE NOTES

ADVANCED TECHNIQUES
---------------------------------------
Deafening Chime Stacking:
1. Reach 40 clicks → Mend Radiant → +5 Deafening
2. Reach 40 clicks again → Mend Radiant → +5 Deafening (10 total)
3. Reach 60 clicks → Mend Harmonizing → 64× note multiplier (uses 6 stacks)
4. Remaining 4 stacks persist for next cycle

Entropic Maximization:
• With Razer equipped, always mend at 60+ clicks for 83 Entropic
• Radiant mends also grant 5 Entropic Crescendo of Eternity critical hit clicks (via Deafening)
• Combine with Fate/Enigma for entropic synergy
