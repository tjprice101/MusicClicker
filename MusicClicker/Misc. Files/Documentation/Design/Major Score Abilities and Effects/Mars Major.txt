===============================================================================
MARS MAJOR: "BLADE OF SYMPHONIC WAR"
===============================================================================

"The blade sings with each strike—a symphony of war echoing through eternity."

OVERVIEW:
=========
Mars is an endgame Boss Fight major score featuring a blade-charging system that 
builds power through clicking, generates consumable stacks, and unleashes 
devastating "Infinite War of Harmony" critical hits. The system rewards both 
sustained clicking and strategic resource management.

===============================================================================
CRESCENDANCE ABILITY: BLADE OF SYMPHONIC WAR
===============================================================================

CORE MECHANIC: BLADE CHARGING
------------------------------
• Every click charges the Blade by 1% (0% to 300% maximum)
• At 100%+: Each 1% of charge generates 1 Resolute Fractal stack
• Blade charge is visible in the Mars Crescendance panel
• Charge rate is QUADRUPLED (4% per click) during Annihilation Nocturne duet

IMPORTANT: Infinite War critical clicks do NOT charge the blade!

RESOURCE 1: BLADE CHARGE (0-300%)
----------------------------------
• Built: 1% per click (4% during Annihilation Nocturne duet)
• Purpose: Powers Infinite War critical hits
• Display: Real-time percentage shown in crescendance panel
• Note: Fully depletes to 0% after all Infinite War crits are spent

RESOURCE 2: RESOLUTE FRACTAL STACKS
------------------------------------
• Generated: When Blade Charge exceeds 100%, each 1% generates 1 stack
• Consumption: 1 stack = Quintuple (5×) current notes
• Special: Every 5th consumption triggers Infinite War mode
• Display: Stack count shown in crescendance panel

RESOURCE 3: OBLIVION'S DESTRUCTION STACKS
------------------------------------------
• Generated: From Fractal of War weapon bond (see weapon section)
• Source: Each "Infinite War of Harmony!!!" critical hit grants +1 stack
• Consumption: 5 stacks = +30 to ALL owned minor scores
• Display: Stack count shown in crescendance panel with white glow effect

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CRESCENDANCE BUTTONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BUTTON 1: Consume Resolute Fractal
-----------------------------------
Cost: 1 Resolute Fractal stack
Effect: Multiply current notes by 5× (quintuple)
Special: Every 5th consumption triggers Infinite War mode:
  • Grants 5 "Infinite War of Harmony!!!" critical hits
  • Each crit deals: NPC × (Blade Charge%)^4
  • After all 5 crits are spent, Blade fully depletes to 0%
Cooldown: None (limited only by stack availability)

BUTTON 2: Consume Oblivion's Destruction
-----------------------------------------
Cost: 5 Oblivion's Destruction stacks
Effect: +30 to ALL owned minor scores (Melodious & Harmonious Fragments)
Cooldown: None (limited only by stack availability)
Requirement: Must own Fractal of War weapon and have it equipped

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INFINITE WAR OF HARMONY CRITICAL HITS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger: Every 5th Resolute Fractal consumed
Quantity: 5 critical hits per trigger
Formula: NPC × (Blade Charge%)^4

Key Mechanics:
• Each critical is 4× more effective than standard formula (power of 4)
• Critical clicks do NOT charge the blade (prevents infinite loop)
• After all 5 crits are spent, Blade Charge resets to 0%
• Each crit generates +1 Oblivion's Destruction (if Fractal of War equipped)

Visual Effects:
• Text: "Infinite War of Harmony!!! +[damage]"
• Color: Deep red text (#8B0000)
• Outline: Black stroke
• Screen Shake: Intensity 1.5, duration 300ms

Example Damage Calculation:
  NPC = 1,000
  Blade Charge = 200%
  Damage = 1,000 × (200)^4 = 1,000 × 1,600,000,000 = 1.6 trillion notes

===============================================================================
WEAPON 1: FRACTAL OF WAR
===============================================================================

PASSIVE: Recursive Devastation
-------------------------------
Trigger: Every 25th click
Effects:
  • Double your Entropic Melodies
  • Grant +10 to ALL 8 upgrades (Chord, Scale, Orchestra, Symphony, 
    Aria, Requiem, Opus, Magnum Opus)

Purpose: Provides consistent upgrade and Entropic Melody scaling
Note: Simple passive that doesn't interact with crescendance stacks

CRESCENDANCE BOND: Fractal Armament
------------------------------------
Requirement: Fractal of War equipped in weapon slot 1 or 2
Effect: Each "Infinite War of Harmony!!!" critical hit grants +1 Oblivion's 
        Destruction stack
Synergy: Enables powerful minor score boosting via Oblivion's Destruction 
         consumption (5 stacks = +30 to all minors)

===============================================================================
WEAPON 2: CONSONANCE'S REQUIEMIC WAR
===============================================================================

PASSIVE: Harmonic Dissolution
------------------------------
Trigger: Every 30th click
Effect: Grant bonus notes equal to (NPC × NPS × 0.1)
Purpose: Provides hybrid scaling that benefits from both clicking and idle gains
Note: Simple passive that doesn't interact with crescendance stacks

CRESCENDANCE BOND: Requiemic Resonance
---------------------------------------
Requirement: Consonance's Requiemic War equipped in weapon slot 1 or 2
Trigger: Each Resolute Fractal OR Oblivion's Destruction consumption
Effect: Next 3 clicks gain NPC^1.56 bonus damage
Visual: "Requiemic Resonance!!! +[damage]"
Color: Firebrick red text (#B22222) with black outline

Formula Example:
  NPC = 1,000
  Bonus = 1,000^1.56 ≈ 39,810,717 notes per click (for 3 clicks)

===============================================================================
DUET RESONANCE: ANNIHILATION NOCTURNE
===============================================================================

Requirement: Fractal of War + Consonance's Requiemic War both equipped
Type: Timed ability (lasts 12 seconds)
Cooldown: 600 seconds (10 minutes)

EFFECTS WHILE ACTIVE:
---------------------
1. Duration: 12 seconds of enhanced clicking
2. Blade Charging: 4× faster (4% per click instead of 1%)
3. Every click is an "Annihilation Nocturne" critical that grants:
   • Bonus notes equal to NPC^(NPC/NPS)
   • +2 to ALL owned major scores
4. Instantly maxes Blade to 300% (grants 200 Resolute Fractal stacks)
5. Visual: "Annihilation Nocturne!!! +[damage]"
   • Color: Black text with deep red outline (#8B0000)
   • Screen Shake: Intensity 1.0, duration 200ms
   • Flame overlay borders the screen

STRATEGY NOTES:
---------------
• Use when Blade is depleted to maximize the instant 300% charge
• Click rapidly during 12-second window for massive note gains and major score boosts
• The NPC^(NPC/NPS) formula rewards balanced NPC/NPS ratios
• Each click boosting ALL owned majors makes this extremely powerful late-game

Formula Example (Bonus Clicks):
  NPC = 10,000
  NPS = 1,000
  Bonus = 10,000^(10,000/1,000) = 10,000^10 = massive damage

===============================================================================
CONSUME ALL & MERGE ALL BUTTONS
===============================================================================

CONSUME ALL (Resolute Fractals):
---------------------------------
Requirement: 5+ Resolute Fractal stacks
Effect: Consumes ALL stacks in groups of 5
  • Each group of 5 quintuples notes and grants 5 Infinite War crits
  • Leftover stacks (less than 5) are preserved
Purpose: Quickly process large numbers of Resolute Fractal stacks
Example: 23 stacks → 4 consumptions (20 stacks used), 3 remaining

MERGE ALL (Infinite War Criticals):
------------------------------------
Requirement: 2+ Infinite War critical hits stored
Effect: Combines ALL stored Infinite War crits into one mega-critical
Result: "Pyroclastic Harmony of War's Eternal Rage!!!" critical hit
  • Formula: NPC^(number of crits merged)
  • Visual: Black and red shimmering text with white shadow
  • Screen Shake: High intensity
Purpose: Convert many small crits into one devastating attack
Example: 15 crits merged → NPC^15 bonus damage

===============================================================================
SYNERGY & STRATEGY
===============================================================================

CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP:
-------------------
1. Click to build Blade Charge (0% → 300%)
2. Charge above 100% generates Resolute Fractal stacks
3. Consume Resolute Fractal for 5× notes multiplier (or use Consume All)
4. Every 5th consumption triggers Infinite War (5 powerful crits)
5. Infinite War crits build Oblivion's Destruction (if Fractal of War equipped)
6. Use Merge All to combine crits into Pyroclastic Harmony mega-attack
7. Consume Oblivion's Destruction for +30 to all minor scores

OPTIMIZATION STRATEGIES:
------------------------

Early Game (Building Stacks):
• Use Annihilation Nocturne duet for 4× blade charging + instant 300%
• Focus on clicking rapidly during 12-second duet window
• Bank Resolute Fractal stacks before consuming

Mid Game (Infinite War Farming):
• Use "Consume All" to quickly process large Resolute Fractal stacks
• Each Infinite War crit grants Oblivion's Destruction
• Build up Oblivion's Destruction to 5+ stacks

Late Game (Mega Damage):
• Use "Merge All" to combine Infinite War crits into Pyroclastic Harmony
• Formula: NPC^(number of crits) = astronomical damage
• Combine with Annihilation Nocturne's +2 to all majors per click

ADVANCED TACTICS:
-----------------
• Delay Infinite War crits until you have many stored, then Merge All
• Use Consonance Requiem bonus immediately after Resolute Fractal consumption
• Annihilation Nocturne's +2 majors per click compounds with owned major count
• Stack Infinite War crits from multiple Consume All operations before merging

===============================================================================
THEMATIC NOTES
===============================================================================

NAMING CONVENTIONS:
-------------------
• "Blade of Symphonic War" - The crescendance represents war as a symphony
• "Resolute Fractal" - Fractured determination, war's geometric precision
• "Oblivion's Destruction" - Ultimate dissolution into musical chaos
• "Infinite War of Harmony" - Endless conflict resolved through musical balance
• "Annihilation Nocturne" - Nighttime destruction, the dark side of harmony
• "Fractal of War" - War's recursive, self-similar nature
• "Consonance's Requiemic War" - Harmonious death, war's final resolution

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY:
------------------
Mars represents war not as chaos, but as a disciplined, methodical force. The 
blade charges with each strike, building toward devastating crescendos. The 
system rewards patience (building to 300% charge) and strategic timing (every 
5th consumption), making Mars a major score about precision and control in 
the midst of combat.
