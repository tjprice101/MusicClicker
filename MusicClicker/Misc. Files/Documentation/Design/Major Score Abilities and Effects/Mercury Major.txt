"Mercury, The Winged Messenger" Major Score
============================================

CRESCENDANCE ABILITIES:

Ability 1: Herald's Descent
----------------------------
• On activate, instantly double your notes and gain 5 stacks of Swift Resonance
• 15 second cooldown

Ability 2: Resonant Transformation
------------------------------------
• On activate, consume 1 stack of Swift Resonance to give 1 stack of Herald's Message and +250 Entropic Melodies.

Ability 3: Symphony's Bounty
-----------------------------
• On activate, consume 3 stacks of Herald's Message and gain +X Entropic Melodies and gain +NPS^25 Notes. 
• X = (number of currently owned minor scores) × 5

CUSTOM STACKS:
--------------
• Swift Resonance: Gained from Herald's Descent (5 per activation)
• Herald's Message: Gained by converting Swift Resonance (1:1 ratio)
• Planetary Alignment: Gained from Weapon 1 Crescendance Bond

WEAPON 1: Mercurial Overture
============================
Passive (on equip):
• Every 3rd click gives your next click (NPC × NPC + (Entropic Melodies × 1,000)) notes

Crescendance Bond:
• Every stack of Herald's Message consumed, give one stack of Planetary Alignment
• Your next 30 clicks gain a new critical "Messenger of Planetary Resonance!!!!"
• Critical formula: (NPS × 30,000) + (NPC × # of Planetary Alignment stacks)

WEAPON 2: Wing of the Messenger
============================
Passive (on equip):
• Every 50th click gives you +X minor scores to your lowest owned minor score
• X = (number of Entropic Melodies owned) ÷ 5

Crescendance Bond:
• Consume Planetary Alignment stacks to give your next 5 clicks a new critical
• Critical: "A Thousand Winged Symphony for the Universe!!!"
• Critical formula: NPC^1000

DUET: Celestial Messengers
===========================
Choose between 3 different abilities to activate:

Divine Proclamation
------------------------------
• Immediately triple your current owned notes
• Double your current owned Entropic Melodies
• 3 minute cooldown

Fate's Grand Message
-------------------------------
• Consume all stacks of Swift Resonance, Herald's Message, and Planetary Alignment
• Boost your next 10 clicks with a new critical: "Messenger of Fate's Melody!!!"
• Critical formula: NPS^(total # of stacks consumed)
• 8 minute cooldown

Sacrificial Ascension
--------------------------------
• Sacrifice 75% of your current notes
• Gain 3 stacks of Swift Resonance, Herald's Message, and Planetary Alignment each
• Gain +25 owned to every upgrade in the upgrade menu
• 5 minute cooldown

DESIGN NOTES:
=============
• Mercury theme: Speed, transformation, messages, planetary motion
• Stack conversion chain: Swift Resonance → Herald's Message → Planetary Alignment
• Weapon 1 focuses on click crits and stack generation
• Weapon 2 focuses on minor score growth and powerful burst crits
• Duet offers strategic choice between immediate power, scaling crits, or long-term investment
