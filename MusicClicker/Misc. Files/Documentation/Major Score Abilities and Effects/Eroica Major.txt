===========================================
EROICA (SYMPHONY NO. 3) MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"A hero's triumph echoes through eternity, where crimson sakura blooms amidst symphonic glory."

CRESCENDANCE: Symphony of Triumph
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Eroica Major must be resonated

Mechanics:
• Every minor craft: +1 Heroic Resolve stack
• Consume 1 Heroic Resolve + 25% current notes:
  - Grants +1 Symphonic Catharsis stack
  
• Consume 1 Symphonic Catharsis:
  - 10 seconds of double NPC
  - +10% critical hit chance for 10 seconds
  - Triggers weapon bonds (see below)

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Sakura's Blossom (Weapon I):
• Forte Resonance - Heroic Bounty (while equipped):
  - On Major acquisition (unite the symphony screen):
    → +1 of each component type (Keys, Scales, Progressions) for that Major's score
  - Trigger: Crafting major score from the unite the symphony screen.
  
• Crescendance Bond - Crimson Requiem:
  - When Symphonic Catharsis consumed: Next 30 clicks gain bonus
  - Bonus per click = NPC + (NPS × NPC)
  - Special crit: "Blossom's Blooming in Crimson Light" (salmon-red text with black border)
  - Requires: Eroica Major resonated + Sakura equipped
  - Extremely powerful burst damage window

Funeral Prayer (Weapon II):
• Forte Resonance - Prayer of Valor (while equipped):
  - Every 10th click: +1 Prayer stack
  - At 3 Prayer stacks: Next 15 clicks each gain +6× NPS as a special critical called "Retribution of the Symphonic Sakura" (dark pink text with black outline)
  - Auto-triggers and resets
  
• Crescendance Bond - Testament of Harmony:
  - When Symphonic Catharsis consumed: +1 Testament of Harmony stack
  - Consume 1 Testament: +100 Melodious + 100 Harmonious Fragments
  - Requires: Eroica Major resonated + Funeral Prayer equipped
  - Manual consumption via button

DUET ABILITY: Victory March (Sakura + Funeral Prayer)
---------------------------------------
Fill a 100-click progress bar for escalating rewards:
• 25% progress (25 clicks): Random minor score
• 50% progress (50 clicks): Random major score
• 75% progress (75 clicks): +50 Melodious + 50 Harmonious Fragments
• 100% progress (100 clicks): Double all owned scores

Duration: N/A (progress-based, not time-based)
Cooldown: 5 minutes

BALANCE NOTES
---------------------------------------
Version 1.3 - Major Ability Removal:
• Removed Eroica Major Ability (Triumph of Heroes) that doubled notes on major acquisition
• Reason: Effect was passive and didn't fit with Eroica's active crescendance theme
• Crescendance renamed from "Heroic Momentum" to "Symphony of Triumph" for clarity

Version 1.2 - Eroica Deep Dive:
• Removed "double NPS beyond 10th minor" effect (was not documented, removed for clarity)
• All Eroica functionality verified and documented accurately

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Heroic Resolve generation requires active minor crafting
2. Save Symphonic Catharsis for burst windows (boss fights, critical moments)
3. Sakura's Crimson Requiem creates massive click damage - combine with click speed buffs
4. Funeral Prayer's Testament provides steady fragment income
5. Victory March duet is one of the strongest score multipliers in the game
6. "Blossom's Blooming in Crimson Light" crits deal NPC + (NPS × NPC) damage - maximize both stats
7. Prayer of Valor's "Retribution" crits grant +6× NPS bonus - powerful passive income boost

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Focus on acquiring both weapons for Victory March access
• Heroic Resolve stacks slowly at first

Mid Game:
• Heroic Resolve stacking becomes reliable with consistent minor crafting
• Testament fragments help accelerate progression
• Prayer passive provides consistent click bonuses

Late Game:
• Crimson Requiem creates extreme burst windows with "Blossom's Blooming in Crimson Light" crits
• Victory March doubling effect becomes exponentially powerful
• Pair with Moonlight/Fate for combo multiplication effects
• Symphonic Catharsis double NPC stacks with other click multipliers
• Prayer of Valor "Retribution" crits provide consistent 6× NPS bursts

SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------------
Best Paired With:
• Moonlight Sonata: Nighttime clicking with Crimson Requiem = massive burst
• Fate: Cosmic Modulation stacks benefit from double NPC window
• La Campanella: Bell cracks during Crimson Requiem accelerate Deafening Chime

Avoid Pairing With:
• Winter: NPS freeze negates Prayer passive and some weapon effects
