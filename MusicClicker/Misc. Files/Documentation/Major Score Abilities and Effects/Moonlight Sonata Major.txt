===========================================
MOONLIGHT SONATA MAJOR - ABILITIES & EFFECTS
===========================================

Flavor Text:
"Beneath the silent moon, shadows dance with luminous grace—nightfall becomes your sanctuary."

CRESCENDANCE: Eclipse of the Nocturne
---------------------------------------
Requirements:
• Moonlight Sonata Major must be resonated
• Active during nighttime (8PM-6AM system time)

Mechanics:
• Every 20th click during nighttime: Gain 1 "Moonbeam Resonance" stack
• At 8 stacks: Auto-consumes all stacks for:
  - +100% of current notes (doubles your total notes)
  - +1 "Harmonizing Moonlight" stack

Harmonizing Moonlight:
• Can be consumed via Eulogy of the Moon weapon
• Consume 1 stack → 3 Moonbeam Resonance + components for lowest minor
• If Moonbeam reaches 8+ from consume: auto-converts to another Harmonizing + bonus notes

WEAPON SYNERGIES
---------------------------------------

Incisor of Moonlight (Weapon I):
• Passive - Moonlight Strike (while equipped):
  - Every 4th click: +3% bonus of current notes
  - During nighttime (8PM-6AM): +10% bonus instead
  
• Crescendance Bond - Piercing Radiance:
  - When Moonbeam Resonance stack is gained: +10% of current notes
  - Requires: Moonlight Major resonated + Incisor equipped

Eulogy of the Moon (Weapon II):
• Passive - Nocturnal Refund (while equipped):
  - Minor craft refund chance: 25% (50% during nighttime)
  - Refunds all materials (Keys, Scales, Progressions, Fragments)
  
• Crescendance Bond - Requiem of Renewal:
  - Consume 1 Harmonizing Moonlight:
    → 3 Moonbeam Resonance stacks
    → +1 of each component for lowest owned minor
  - Requires: Moonlight Major resonated + Eulogy equipped
  - Manual button on main screen when stacks available

DUET ABILITY: Lunar Phases (Incisor + Eulogy)
---------------------------------------
Cycles through moon phases granting different bonuses:
• New Moon: 2× NPC
• Crescent Moon: 10% component drop chance on clicks
• Full Moon: 3× NPS
• Waning Moon: 50% upgrade cost discount

Special Effect:
• If Moonlight Sonata Major is resonated: ALL phases activate simultaneously!

Duration: 20 seconds
Cooldown: 4 minutes

BALANCE CHANGES (Latest)
---------------------------------------
Version 1.2 - Scaling Adjustments:
• Moonbeam Resonance: Reduced from +250% → +100% notes
• Incisor passive: Reduced from +5%/+20% → +3%/+10%
• Incisor crescendance: Reduced from +20% → +10% per stack

Reason: Moonlight effects were exponentially scaling and becoming too powerful
in late-game scenarios, overshadowing other major scores.

STRATEGY TIPS
---------------------------------------
1. Best used during nighttime (8PM-6AM) for maximum benefit
2. Incisor for aggressive clicking strategies with nighttime bonuses
3. Eulogy for resource generation and refunds
4. Duet ability becomes extremely powerful with Major resonated
5. Use Harmonizing Moonlight strategically - save for when you need
   specific minor components

PROGRESSION NOTES
---------------------------------------
Early Game:
• Crescendance requires nighttime patience
• Focus on getting Incisor weapon for passive bonuses

Mid Game:
• Incisor passive becomes noticeable with higher note totals
• Eulogy refunds help accelerate minor score acquisition

Late Game:
• Duet ability with all phases active is extremely powerful
• Crescendance stacking creates significant note multiplication
• Pair with other major abilities for combo effects
