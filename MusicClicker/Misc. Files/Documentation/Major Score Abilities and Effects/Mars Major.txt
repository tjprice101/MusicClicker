===========================================
MARS MAJOR - BLADE OF SYMPHONIC WAR
===========================================

CRESCENDANCE ABILITY: Blade of Symphonic War
-------------------------------------------
- Every click charges the Blade by 1%
- At 100% charge: Blade continues charging up to 300%
- Above 100%: Each 1% charge generates 1 Resolute Fractal stack
- Maximum charge: 300% (generates 200 Resolute Fractal stacks total)

RESOLUTE FRACTAL CONSUMPTION:
- Cost: 1 Resolute Fractal
- Effect: Quintuple current notes (5× multiplier)
- SPECIAL: Every 5th consumption grants 20 "Infinite War of Harmony!!" critical hits
- Triggers Consonance's Requiemic War bond (see below)

INFINITE WAR OF HARMONY CRITS:
- Granted: 20 crits every 5 Resolute Fractal consumed
- Formula: NPC × (Blade charge %)
- Visual: Deep red text with black outline, intense screen shake
- Special Effect: Each crit grants +1 Oblivion's Destruction stack (if Fractal of War is equipped)
- BLADE DEPLETION: After all Infinite War crits are spent, blade charge resets to 0%

OBLIVION'S DESTRUCTION CONSUMPTION:
- Cost: 5 Oblivion's Destruction stacks
- Effect: +30 to ALL owned minor and major scores
- Triggers Consonance's Requiemic War bond (see below)

===========================================
WEAPON 1: FRACTAL OF WAR
===========================================

PASSIVE ABILITY: Recursive Devastation
-------------------------------------------
- Every 25th click triggers:
  • Doubles Entropic Melodies (EM × 2)
  • Grants +10 to ALL 8 upgrades (Chord, Scale, Orchestra, Symphony, Aria, Requiem, Opus, Magnum Opus)

BOND ABILITY: Fractal Armament
-------------------------------------------
- Effect: Each "Infinite War of Harmony!!" critical hit grants +1 Oblivion's Destruction stack
- Synergy: Enables powerful score boosting via Oblivion's Destruction consumption

===========================================
WEAPON 2: CONSONANCE'S REQUIEMIC WAR
===========================================

PASSIVE ABILITY: Requiem's Crescendo
-------------------------------------------
- Triggers when ANY upgrade is purchased
- Effect: +5% current notes
- Scales multiplicatively with frequent upgrade purchases

BOND ABILITY: Requiemic Resonance
-------------------------------------------
- Triggers when consuming Resolute Fractal OR Oblivion's Destruction
- Effect: Next 3 clicks receive NPC^1.56 bonus
- Visual: "Requiemic Resonance!!!" in firebrick red with black outline

===========================================
DUET ABILITY: ANNIHILATION NOCTURNE
===========================================

EFFECT:
- Duration: 12 seconds
- Every click during the duration is an "Annihilation Nocturne" critical that grants:
  • Bonus notes equal to NPC^(NPC/NPS)
  • +2 to ALL owned major scores per click
- Fully charges Blade to 300% (instantly grants 200 Resolute Fractal stacks)
- Quadruple Resolute Fractal generation during duet (4 stacks per click)

VISUAL:
- "Annihilation Nocturne!!!" text in black with deep red outline
- Heavy screen shake effect
- Flame overlay borders the screen during active duet

COOLDOWN: 600 seconds (10 minutes)

STRATEGIC USE:
- Best used when Blade is empty to maximize the instant 300% charge
- Click rapidly during the 12-second window for massive note gains
- Each click boosts ALL owned major scores, making this a powerful late-game ability
- Combine with weapon passives for maximum effectiveness

===========================================
CONSUME ALL & MERGE ALL BUTTONS
===========================================

CONSUME ALL (Resolute Fractals):
- Requires: 5+ Resolute Fractal stacks
- Effect: Consumes ALL stacks in groups of 5
  • Each group of 5 quintuples notes and grants 5 Infinite War crits
  • Leftover stacks (less than 5) are preserved
- Example: 23 stacks → 4 consumptions (20 stacks used), 3 remaining

MERGE ALL (Infinite War Criticals):
- Requires: 2+ Infinite War critical hits stored
- Effect: Combines ALL stored Infinite War crits into one mega-critical
- Result: "Pyroclastic Harmony of War's Eternal Rage!!!" critical hit
  • Formula: NPC^(number of crits merged)
  • Visual: Black and red shimmering text with white shadow
- Example: 15 crits merged → NPC^15 bonus damage

===========================================
SYNERGY OVERVIEW
===========================================

BLADE CHARGING LOOP:
1. Click to charge Blade from 0% → 300%
2. Generate 200 Resolute Fractal stacks during charging
3. Consume Resolute Fractals for 5× notes (every 5th gives 20 Infinite War crits)
4. Infinite War crits generate Oblivion's Destruction (Fractal of War bond)
5. After Infinite War crits end, Blade depletes to 0%
6. Repeat

WEAPON SYNERGIES:
- Fractal of War: Every 25 clicks → Double EM + +10 all upgrades
  → Builds Oblivion's Destruction via Infinite War crits
- Consonance's Requiemic War: Every upgrade purchase → +5% notes
  → Every Resolute Fractal/Oblivion consumption → 3 clicks with NPC^1.56

DUET SYNERGY:
- Annihilation Nocturne instantly maxes Blade → 200 stacks
- Duration: 12 seconds of NPC^(NPC/NPS) clicks
- Each click grants +2 to ALL owned major scores
- Quadruple generation during duet → massive additional stacks
- Use "Consume All" to quickly process all Resolute Fractal stacks
- Use "Merge All" to combine Infinite War crits into Pyroclastic Harmony

OPTIMAL ROTATION:
1. Use Fractal of War to build upgrades and EM
2. Use Consonance to gain notes from upgrade purchases
3. Charge Blade naturally through clicking
4. Consume Resolute Fractals to quintuple notes (or use Consume All)
5. Every 5th consumption triggers Infinite War sequence
6. Use "Merge All" for massive Pyroclastic Harmony damage
7. Use Oblivion's Destruction for major score boosts
8. Activate Annihilation Nocturne when Blade is depleted for massive reset

===========================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
===========================================

BLADE OF SYMPHONIC WAR represents a **charging crescendo system**:
- Gradual buildup (0-300% charge)
- Explosive payoff (Infinite War crits)
- Strategic depletion (blade resets after crits)
- Cyclical gameplay (charge → discharge → repeat)

The design emphasizes:
- RHYTHM: 25-click weapon passive intervals
- RESONANCE: 5th consumption triggers special effects
- CRESCENDO: Building to Infinite War climax
- RESET: Blade depletion forces strategic timing

Musical Themes:
- "Blade" = Sharp, decisive musical strikes
- "Symphonic War" = Orchestrated conflict and harmony
- "Resolute Fractal" = Repeating patterns that build intensity
- "Infinite War of Harmony" = Endless musical battle reaching peak intensity
- "Annihilation Nocturne" = Night song of destruction
- "Consonance" = Harmonious intervals and resolution
- "Oblivion's Destruction" = Ultimate dissolution into musical chaos
