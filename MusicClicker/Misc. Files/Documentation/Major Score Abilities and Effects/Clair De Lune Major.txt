Clair De Lune Kit:

Crescendance (Main Resonate in Tempo Resonate Screen) 
• Passive: 12PM to 11PM will provide a different benefit every 3 hours. This effect will work retroactively with the current hour range. (Example, 3:00PM to 6:00PM effect will also activate during 3:00AM to 6:00 AM) 
• 12:00 to 3:00 - Every 12th click gives x12,000 NPC. 
• 3:00 to 6:00 - Every 6th click gives 1 stack of Shattered Moonlight. 
• 6:00 to 9:00 - Every 9th click gives 1 stack of Clockwork of Infinity. 
• 9:00 to 12:00 - Every 12th click gives +20% of your current notes. 

Buttons that are always available to use:
• Consume 1 "Shattered Moonlight" : Gives +100 Entropic Melodies. 
• Consume 1 "Clockwork of Infinity" : Gives your next 5 clicks a new critical called "Infinite Temporality!!!" which gives +X notes where X is equal to your (NPC^12). (Critical has a dark gray shadow with a red font) 

Celestial Horology: 
• Passive: Every 5th click gives +3% Entropic Melodies. 
• Crescendance Bond: When Clair De Lune is resonated, every 5th consume of "Shattered Moonlight" gives 1 stack of "Temporal Fracture." 
• Consume 1 stack of "Temporal Fracture" : Instantly give yourself +15% of your current notes. 

Clockwork Finality: 
• Passive: Anytime you dissolve a Major score, give double the amount of Entropic Melodies that it would've given. 
• Crescendance Bond: When Clair De Lune is resonated, anytime you consume "Temporal Fracture", give your next 10 clicks a new critical called "Surge of Time's Fractalization!!!" which gives notes equal to (NPC^144). (Critical has a red shadow with gray text). 

Duet Resonance: 
"Chain of Temporality" 
For the next 12 seconds, each click gives a stacking effect to double your current NPC until the timer ends.
