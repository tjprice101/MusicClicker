===================================================
           CLAIR DE LUNE MAJOR SCORE
===================================================

CRECENDANCE ABILITY: Lunar Chronometry
---------------------------------------------------

CORE MECHANIC - Clock Configuration:
You choose where the hour and minute hands point on a clock face.
Each position provides unique effects and stack mechanics, themed to shattered time and musical resonance.

---------------------------------------------------
HOUR HAND POSITIONS (Effects):
---------------------------------------------------

Hour Hand on 12 (Celestial Assembly):
• Every 3rd, 6th, 9th, and 12th click give stacks of "Chrono Fragments"
• Stacks accumulate: Click 3 gives 1, click 6 gives 2, click 9 gives 2, click 12 gives 4

Hour Hand on 3 (Nocturnal Exchange):
• Consume 1 stack of Chrono Fragments to give yourself:
  - +50% Notes to your current notes
  - +2% Entropic Melodies

Hour Hand on 6 (Fractal Modulation):
• Every 6th click gives you 1 stack of "Twilight Resonance" (max 50 stacks)

Hour Hand on 9 (Syncopated Rupture):
• Consume 1 stack of Twilight Resonance to give your next 10 clicks a special critical:
  - "Twilight Rupture"
  - Gives +X notes where X = ((NPS^9)/5)

---------------------------------------------------
MINUTE HAND POSITIONS (Criticals/Effects):
---------------------------------------------------

Minute Hand on 1 (Cadenza Cataclysm):
• On gain of Twilight Resonance stack, your next 3 clicks will give you +1.5% Entropic Melodies

Minute Hand on 2 (Infinity Arpeggio):
• On gain of Chrono Fragments stack, your next 2 clicks are guaranteed "Timeless Melody" critical

Minute Hand on 4 (Prismal Crescendo):
• Every time you gain Entropic Melodies, increase your current notes by 0.1% per Entropic Melodies gained

Minute Hand on 5 (Refrain of Endless Measures):
• Consume 3 stacks of Chrono Fragments to give yourself +5 to all owned minor scores

Minute Hand on 7 (Sonata of Shattered Hours):
• Consume 20 stacks of Chrono Fragments to give yourself:
  - 1 Major Score
  - +15% Entropic Melodies

Minute Hand on 8 (Duet: Symphony of Broken Time):
• Consume 8 stacks of Twilight Resonance to give yourself +5 to every owned major

Minute Hand on 10 (Power Spike):
• For each Chrono Fragment consumed, your next click gives +X notes
  - X = (NPC^# of stacks consumed prior to this click)

Minute Hand on 11 (Glass Forte):
• If your notes per second exceeds 1 Quintillion, every 12th click gives you 1 stack of "Perpetual Nocturne"

---------------------------------------------------
CUSTOM STACKS:
---------------------------------------------------

Chrono Fragments:
• Gained from Hour Hand on 12 position (every 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th click)
• Can be consumed by various Minute Hand positions for powerful effects

Twilight Resonance:
• Gained from Hour Hand on 6 position (every 6th click)
• Can be consumed for Twilight Rupture criticals and other effects

Perpetual Nocturne:
• Gained from Minute Hand on 11 position (requires 1 Quintillion+ NPS)
• Used by Celestial Horology's Crescendance Bond

===================================================
           WEAPON 1: CELESTIAL HOROLOGY
===================================================

PASSIVE ON EQUIP: Chrono-Entropic Refund
• Anytime you spend Entropic Melodies, refund 50% of them

CRESCENDANCE BOND: Celestial Convergence
• Consume 1 stack of Perpetual Nocturne to give your next 2 clicks a new critical:
  - "Lunar Everlasting"
  - Gives ((NPS^NPC)/(144)) notes

===================================================
           WEAPON 2: METRONOMIC DISSONANCE
===================================================

PASSIVE ON EQUIP: Chrono-Refraction
• Anytime a custom stack is consumed, 10% chance to refund that stack
  (Or stacks if multiple are consumed)

CRESCENDANCE BOND: Cascading Resonance
• Every time you gain a stack of Chrono Fragments:
  - Gain +12 of 3 random owned minors

• Every time you gain a stack of Twilight Resonance:
  - Gain +2 to 5 random owned majors
  - (Not including Event majors or Discordance majors)

===================================================
           DUET ABILITY: LUNAR DODECAPHONY
===================================================
Duet Name: Clair de Lune's Embrace

SEQUENTIAL CLICK EFFECTS:

1st Click:
• Triples your current owned notes

2nd Click:
• Gives +75% to your current owned Entropic Melodies

3rd Click:
• Gives +5 to a random owned minor

4th Click:
• Gives +5 to a random owned major
  (Not including Event or Discordance majors)

5th Click:
• Consumes all stacks of Chrono Fragments and Twilight Resonance
• Gives yourself (NPC * (# of stacks consumed)^5) notes

6th Click:
• Gives +5 stacks of Chrono Fragments

7th Click:
• Gives +5% Entropic Melodies

8th Click:
• Gives +1 stack of Twilight Resonance

9th Click:
• Makes your next 3 clicks "Entropic Crescendo of Eternity" Criticals

10th Click:
• Gives you +50 Harmonious and Melodious Fragments

11th Click:
• Gives you +2 Clair De Lune Major

12th Click:
• Gives your next 5 clicks a new critical: "Eternal Moonlight Opus"
• Gives you +X notes per click where X = (NPC^NPS^12)

===================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES:
===================================================

• All criticals and effects are stackable
• Clock hand positions can be changed/customized
• Custom stacks persist until consumed
• Weapon names pending (currently WEAPON 1 and WEAPON 2)
