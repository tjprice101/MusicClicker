===================================================
           CLAIR DE LUNE MAJOR SCORE
===================================================

CRESCENDANCE ABILITY: Clockwork Symphony
---------------------------------------------------

CORE MECHANIC - Clock Configuration:
You choose where the hour and minute hands point on a clock face.
Each position provides unique effects and stack mechanics.

---------------------------------------------------
HOUR HAND POSITIONS:
---------------------------------------------------

Hour Hand on 12:
• Every 3rd, 6th, 9th, and 12th click give 1 stack of "Clockwork Forte"

Hour Hand on 3:
• Consume 1 stack of Clockwork Forte to give yourself:
  - +50% Notes to your current notes
  - +2% Entropic Melodies

Hour Hand on 6:
• Every 6th click gives you 1 stack of "Temporal Harmony"

Hour Hand on 9:
• Consume 1 stack of Temporal Harmony to give your next 10 clicks a special critical:
  - "Era of the Music's Chronos"
  - Gives +X notes where X = ((NPS^9)/5)

---------------------------------------------------
MINUTE HAND POSITIONS:
---------------------------------------------------

Minute Hand on 1:
• On gain of Temporal Harmony stack, your next 3 clicks will give you +1.5% Entropic Melodies

Minute Hand on 2:
• On gain of Clockwork Forte stack, your next 2 clicks are guaranteed "Entropic Crescendo of Eternity" critical

Minute Hand on 4:
• Every time you gain Entropic Melodies, increase your current notes by 0.1% per Entropic Melodies gained

Minute Hand on 5:
• Consume 3 stacks of Clockwork Forte to give yourself +5 to all owned minor scores

Minute Hand on 7:
• Consume 20 stacks of Clockwork Forte to give yourself:
  - 1 Clair De Lune Major
  - +15% Entropic Melodies

Minute Hand on 8:
• Consume 8 stacks of Temporal Harmony to give yourself +5 to every owned major

Minute Hand on 10:
• For each Clockwork Forte stack consumed, your next click gives +X notes
  - X = (NPC^# of stacks consumed prior to this click)

Minute Hand on 11:
• If your notes per second exceeds 1 Quintillion, every 12th click gives you 1 stack of "Clock of Eternity"

---------------------------------------------------
CUSTOM STACKS:
---------------------------------------------------

Clockwork Forte:
• Gained from Hour Hand on 12 position (every 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th click)
• Can be consumed by various Minute Hand positions for powerful effects

Temporal Harmony:
• Gained from Hour Hand on 6 position (every 6th click)
• Can be consumed for Era of the Music's Chronos criticals and other effects

Clock of Eternity:
• Gained from Minute Hand on 11 position (requires 1 Quintillion+ NPS)
• Used by Weapon 1's Crescendance Bond

===================================================
           WEAPON 1
===================================================

PASSIVE ON EQUIP:
• Anytime you spend Entropic Melodies, refund 50% of them

CRESCENDANCE BOND:
• Consume 1 stack of Clock of Eternity to give your next 2 clicks a new critical:
  - "Everlasting Melody of Time's Infinite Symphony!!!"
  - Gives ((NPS^NPC)/(144)) notes

===================================================
           WEAPON 2
===================================================

PASSIVE ON EQUIP:
• Anytime a custom stack is consumed, 10% chance to refund that stack
  (Or stacks if multiple are consumed)

CRESCENDANCE BOND:
• Every time you gain a stack of Clockwork Forte:
  - Gain +12 of 3 random owned minors

• Every time you gain a stack of Temporal Harmony:
  - Gain +2 to 5 random owned majors
  - (Not including Event majors or Discordance majors)

===================================================
           DUET ABILITY
===================================================

SEQUENTIAL CLICK EFFECTS:

1st Click:
• Triples your current owned notes

2nd Click:
• Gives +75% to your current owned Entropic Melodies

3rd Click:
• Gives +5 to a random owned minor

4th Click:
• Gives +5 to a random owned major
  (Not including Event or Discordance majors)

5th Click:
• Consumes all stacks of Clockwork Forte and Temporal Harmony
• Gives yourself (NPC * (# of stacks consumed)^5) notes

6th Click:
• Gives +5 stacks of Clockwork Forte

7th Click:
• Gives +5% Entropic Melodies

8th Click:
• Gives +1 stack of Temporal Harmony

9th Click:
• Makes your next 3 clicks "Entropic Crescendo of Eternity" Criticals

10th Click:
• Gives you +50 Harmonious and Melodious Fragments

11th Click:
• Gives you +2 Clair De Lune Major

12th Click:
• Gives your next 5 clicks a new critical: "Symphony of Infinity"
• Gives you +X notes per click where X = (NPC^NPS^12)

===================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES:
===================================================

• All criticals and effects are stackable
• Clock hand positions can be changed/customized
• Custom stacks persist until consumed
• Weapon names pending (currently WEAPON 1 and WEAPON 2)
