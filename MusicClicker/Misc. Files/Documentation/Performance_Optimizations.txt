SECTION 2: OVERFLOW PROTECTION
================================================================================

2.1 AtomicDouble OVERFLOW CAPPING (AtomicDouble.cs)
---------------------------------------------------
Added:
- MaxSafeValue constant = 1e300 (leaves headroom below double.MaxValue ~1.79e308)
- Input validation: if value is infinity/NaN, cap to MaxSafeValue * 0.01
- Result clamping: if computed > MaxSafeValue or infinity/NaN, cap to MaxSafeValue
- SafeValue() helper method for pre-calculation overflow checking

2.2 NumberFormatter OVERFLOW HANDLING (NumberFormatter.cs)
----------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE: Returns "∞" for infinity/NaN values
AFTER: Caps to MaxSafeValue and formats normally (never shows "∞")

Changed:
- Instead of returning "∞", caps number to AtomicDouble.MaxSafeValue
- Values > MaxSafeValue are also capped before formatting

================================================================================
SECTION 3: CRITICAL FORMULA REBALANCING
================================================================================

All formulas changed from exponential (value^power) to logarithmic (value × log(value)^power)
This prevents overflow while maintaining meaningful progression.

3.1 INFERNAL SYMPHONY OF OBLIVION (MainWindow.axaml.cs)
-------------------------------------------------------
Location: Dies Irae Duet (Note-Doubling Clicks)
BEFORE: finalNotes = gameState.Notes * 2  (DANGEROUS: doubles current notes)
AFTER:  finalNotes = NPC × NPS × log₁₀(Notes+1)^8

Rationale: Old formula could cause exponential growth when chained. New formula
scales with progress logarithmically.

3.2 BLIZZARD'S COMMAND OF ETERNAL ICE (MainWindow.axaml.cs)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Location: Winter enhanced clicks
BEFORE: finalNotes = (NPS × NPC)²  (DANGEROUS: squared product)
AFTER:  finalNotes = NPC × NPS × log₁₀(NPC×NPS+1)^10

Rationale: Product squared grows extremely fast. Log scaling maintains power fantasy.

3.3 PYROCLASTIC HARMONY OF WAR'S ETERNAL RAGE (MainWindow.axaml.cs)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: MainMergeAllInfiniteWar_Click
BEFORE: bonus = NPC^(mergedCount)  (VERY DANGEROUS: uncapped exponent!)
AFTER:  bonus = NPC × log₁₀(NPC+1)^(min(mergedCount, 50)) × mergedCount

Rationale: With high NPC values (1e50+) and even moderate merge counts (10+),
old formula would immediately overflow to infinity.

3.4 MESSENGER OF FATE'S MELODY (MainWindow.axaml.cs)
----------------------------------------------------
Location: Mercury old Duet crits
BEFORE: finalNotes = NPS^10  (DANGEROUS: hardcoded high power)
AFTER:  finalNotes = NPS × NPC × log₁₀(NPS+1)^10

Rationale: NPS^10 with high NPS values overflows quickly.

3.5 ANNIHILATION NOCTURNE (MainWindow.axaml.cs)
-----------------------------------------------
Location: Mars Duet
BEFORE: annihilationBonus = NPC^(cappedRatio)  where cappedRatio ≤ 10
AFTER:  annihilationBonus = NPC × log₁₀(NPC+1)^(cappedRatio)

Rationale: Even NPC^10 can overflow with high NPC values.

3.6 INFINITE WAR OF HARMONY (WeaponAbilities.cs)
------------------------------------------------
Location: Mars_GetInfiniteWarOfHarmonyCritDamage
BEFORE: return NPC × BladeCharge^4  (DANGEROUS: ^4 with high charge)
AFTER:  return NPC × BladeCharge × log₁₀(BladeCharge+1)^4

Rationale: Blade charge can reach very high values, causing overflow.

3.7 MERCURY MAJOR ABILITY - NPS BONUS (WeaponAbilities.cs)
----------------------------------------------------------
Location: Mercury_MajorAbility_ConsumeHeraldsMessage
BEFORE: npsBonus = NPS^25  (VERY DANGEROUS: ^25 power!)
AFTER:  npsBonus = NPS × NPC × log₁₀(NPS+1)^25

Rationale: Any NPS > ~2.5 with ^25 power immediately overflows.

3.8 CLAIR DE LUNE CRESCENDANCE PASSIVE (Progression.cs)
-------------------------------------------------------
Location: CalculateNotesPerClick
BEFORE: npsBonus = NPS^5
AFTER:  npsBonus = NPS × log₁₀(NPS+1)^5

Rationale: Applied every NPC calculation, so must be stable.
