# Major Score Resonance Effect Formulas
# =====================================
# This document lists all the scaled-down formulas used for major score resonance effects.
# These formulas were adjusted from their original extreme values to prevent "infinite" display issues.
# Last Updated: January 1, 2026 - Second Nerf Pass

## CLAIR DE LUNE MAJOR SCORE
## -------------------------

### Infinite Temporality (Clockwork of Infinity consume)
- ORIGINAL FORMULA: NPC^12
- FIRST NERF: NPC × log₁₀(NPC + 1)^12
- CURRENT FORMULA: NPC × log₁₀(NPC + 1)^8
- RATIONALE: Further reduced exponent for more gradual progression

### Surge of Time's Fractalization (Temporal Fracture consume with Clockwork Finality bond)
- ORIGINAL FORMULA: NPC^144
- FIRST NERF: NPC × NPS × log₁₀(NPC + 1)^24
- CURRENT FORMULA: NPC × NPS × log₁₀(NPC + 1)^12
- RATIONALE: Halved exponent for more reasonable late-game scaling


## MERCURY MAJOR SCORE
## -------------------

### Messenger of Planetary Resonance (Mercurial Overture bond - 5 clicks, was 30)
- ORIGINAL FORMULA: (NPS × 30,000) + (NPC × Planetary Alignment stacks)
- CURRENT FORMULA: (NPS × 5,000) + (NPC × log₁₀(NPC+1)^8 × Planetary Alignment stacks)
- CLICK COUNT: Reduced from 30 to 5 critical hits per Herald's Message consumed
- RATIONALE: Log-based scaling with stacks provides meaningful growth without overflow

### A Thousand Winged Symphony for the Universe (Wing of the Messenger bond - consume Planetary Alignment)
- ORIGINAL FORMULA: NPC^1000
- FIRST NERF: NPC × NPS × log₁₀(NPC + 1)^50 × (1 + stacks)
- CURRENT FORMULA: NPC × NPS × log₁₀(NPC + 1)^20 × (1 + Planetary Alignment stacks)
- RATIONALE: Reduced exponent from 50 to 20 for less extreme scaling

### Alignment of Mercurial Judgement (Mercury Duet - Swift Delivery active)
- ORIGINAL FORMULA: ((NPC × NPS)^stacks)
- FIRST NERF: ((NPC × NPS)^stacks) capped at 50
- CURRENT FORMULA: NPC × NPS × log₁₀(NPC+1)^stacks (capped at 30)
- RATIONALE: Changed from exponential base to log-based exponent for controlled growth
- NOTE: Each click during 30-second duration grants +1 Swift Resonance stack


## MARS MAJOR SCORE
## ----------------

### Annihilation Nocturne (Mars Duet - time-based)
- ORIGINAL FORMULA: NPC^(NPC/NPS) with no cap
- CURRENT FORMULA: NPC^(min(NPC/NPS, 10)) + bonus to all majors
- CAP: NPC/NPS ratio capped at 10 to prevent extreme exponents
- RATIONALE: When NPC >> NPS, the ratio could become very large, causing overflow

### Consonance's Requiemic War (Mars Duet bonus clicks)
- FORMULA: NPC^1.56
- STATUS: UNCHANGED - This formula was already reasonable


## UNCHANGED FORMULAS (Reference)
## =============================

### Messenger of Fate's Melody (old Duet - Fate's Grand Message)
- FORMULA: NPS^10 (fixed power based on typical stack counts)
- STATUS: Reasonable for late-game


## FORMULA PROGRESSION HIERARCHY
## =============================
# Damage increases as you progress through the weapon/ability chain:

CLAIR DE LUNE PROGRESSION:
1. Infinite Temporality: NPC × log^8 (base ability)
2. Surge of Time's Fractalization: NPC × NPS × log^12 (bond upgrade)

MERCURY PROGRESSION:
1. Messenger of Planetary Resonance: (NPS × 5K) + (NPC × log^8 × stacks) (5 hits)
2. Alignment of Mercurial Judgement: NPC × NPS × log^stacks (during duet)
3. A Thousand Winged Symphony: NPC × NPS × log^20 × stacks (consume stacks)

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
## =================
1. Use logarithmic scaling (log₁₀) for very large exponents to create meaningful but bounded growth
2. Incorporate multiple stats (NPC, NPS, stacks) for more interesting interactions
3. Cap ratio-based exponents to prevent edge cases
4. Maintain thematic consistency (e.g., Thousand Winged Symphony still uses Planetary Alignment)
5. Ensure late-game content remains powerful but never displays "infinite"
6. Create clear progression: base abilities < bond upgrades < duet abilities < stack consumption
